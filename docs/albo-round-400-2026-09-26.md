# Albo round 400: the e's bar sits 2 units higher, and the e gets a gate (2026-09-26)

Owner ruling, 2026-09-26: **"Ship A"**. Arm A is from
`docs/albo-e-legibility-2026-09-26.md`.

- **Baseline:** round 399 (`1648143`, tree at `d21ea24`).
- **Units:** font units (upem 1000, x-height 429).

## 1. What changed

| dial | file | round 399 | round 400 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ALBO_ROM_E_BAR_TOP` (roman e bar top, × xh) | `outlines/glyphs/rounds.py:249` | 0.58 (round 92's `E_BAR_ADJ`, not a dial then) | **0.585** |
| `ALBO_ROM_E_BAR_TAPER` (bar underside rising to the mouth) | `rounds.py:250` | — | 0 (new, inert) |
| `ALBO_ROM_E_BAR_FLOOR` | `rounds.py:232` | 0.45 | **0.45, his pick, unchanged** |

**Why:** round 395's 0.45 floor put the e's outline in a window where
FreeType's autohinter inks the e's mouth at 8–9.75 ppem. Apple Vision then
read the e as o in the Kept Legibility Index. Raising the bar 2.1 units moves
the outline out of that window and keeps the bar's thickness exactly. The
full trace is in the e-legibility doc.

**Scope:** both dials apply to the roman at or under stem 84, like the floor.
The Bold keeps 0.58.

## 2. What moved (`cmp_outlines.py --verbose --advances`, round 399 → 400, all four cuts built with `build_env.sh`)

| cut | outlines moved | advances |
|---|---|---|
| Regular | `e`, `ae`, `oe` (3 of 530) | none |
| Italic | 0 | none |
| Bold | 0 | none |
| BoldItalic | 0 | none |

- **The accented e's follow by reference.** `è é ê ë ē ĕ ė ę ě` are
  composites on `e`, and their component offsets are unchanged in the
  Regular and the Bold. The accents do not move; A2 would have moved them 1
  unit.
- **`æ œ`** are drawn glyphs and moved with the e.
- **GPOS, GSUB, hmtx and cmap** are identical in the Regular and the Bold.
  Kerning and advances are unchanged, so B2 needs nothing.
- **The e's shape** (design units): the bar is 33 at the eye column, as
  before. Its underside goes 217 → 219, the eye 159 → 157, and the mouth
  141 → 144.

**The Bold:** the dial does not apply at 700 (`_heavy()`, S 116 > 84), and
the Bold e is identical to round 399. Its e is not in the window: Vision
reads **7 e>o** and Tesseract 0. The new gate does FAIL the Bold (worst 0.51
at 8.5 ppem), but that is a false positive. The gate's 0.15 limit is
calibrated on the 400's e, and a heavier e fills more of its mouth band at
8 px without reading as o. So `gates.sh` runs the gate on the Regular only,
and says why.

## 5. The gate, wired

`gates.sh` now runs `etrace/e_hint_gate.py` on the built Regular. It is a
hard pass/fail, not a baseline row, because the Regular is clean and must stay
clean.

- **What it measures:** at every quarter ppem from 8 to 12, the lightest
  pixel row in the drawn mouth band. The limit is 0.15. On the trace's 16
  builds, clean fonts read 0.05–0.13 and failing ones 0.20–0.26.
- **Rewritten for the system python:** the gate now uses only numpy and
  freetype, because `gates.sh` runs the system python3, which has no scipy.
  It gives the same value and verdict as the scipy version on all 16 trace
  builds.
