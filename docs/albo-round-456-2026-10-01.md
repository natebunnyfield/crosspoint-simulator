# Albo round 456: ligatures only where they keep the stem rhythm; the italic mark rule at 0.95 (2026-10-01)

## Ligatures and stem rhythm

Owner: *"don't do ligatures if they aren't aligned with stem rhythm of words"*.

**Measured** (`tools/wedge_serif/instruments/lig_rhythm.py`, new): every stem's x where the ink runs through the whole middle band of the x-height (0.35-0.65 of it), on the same text shaped with `liga` on and off. The face's own rhythm is the median of its between-letter stem intervals over plain stem letters (the last stem of the first letter to the first stem of the second: il li ii ll ni in ln nl hi ih mi im ui iu lu ul): Regular 272 units (range 250-289), Bold 321 (300-338), Italic 266, Bold Italic 311. A ligature keeps the rhythm when every stem interval inside it lies within 10 units of that median (10 = the owner's own re-ask repeatability, 10.83).

| | Regular: interval inside the ligature | vs 272 | Bold | vs 321 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ff | 247 | −26 | 319 | −2 |
| fi | 247 | −26 | 319 | −2 |
| fl | 272 | 0 | 322 | 0 |
| ffi | 247, 248 | −26, −25 | 319, 319 | −2, −2 |
| ffl | 247, 273 | −26, 0 | 319, 322 | −2, 0 |

In the Regular the ff, fi and ffi ligatures tuck the next stem 26 units under the face's rhythm; ffl carries the same ff join. The Bold's all sit within 2 units. The same letters set SEPARATELY are looser still (Regular f to i 369, f to f 358, f to l 410): the f's arm and hook fill that interval, which is why a separate f reads in rhythm where a tucked ligature does not.

**The rule now** (`outlines/kern.py` `feature_text`): the Regular substitutes only **fl**; the Bold keeps all five; the italics none (round 309). Every ligature glyph stays drawn and encoded. **The gate:** `lig_rhythm.py --check FONT` exits 1 when a ligature the font substitutes is out of rhythm; `gates.sh` reports it as `ligrhythm.<style>` (the out-of-rhythm list, or none). Before this round it would have read `ff fi ffi ffl` for the Regular.

**Checked:** with fi, ff, ffi and ffl now set as separate glyphs in the Regular, the touch sweeps (`cmp_touch.py`, default and `--composites`) still read 0 under the floor, and no clearance kern was needed. Words: "baffle" and "shuffle" now set f + the fl ligature.

**Not done, offered:** respacing the Regular's ff / fi / ffi ligatures 26 units wider inside would bring them into rhythm and keep them.
