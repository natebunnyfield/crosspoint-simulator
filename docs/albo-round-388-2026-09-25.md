# Albo round 388: six kerns from the re-ask bench, and tracking options (2026-09-25)

Two owner rulings on 2026-09-25, answering the two findings in
`docs/albo-kerning-noise-floor-2026-09-25.md` (RESULT section):

1. **"Set all six."** Six pairs where his two answers agree with each other and
   the model misses by 33–53 units get explicit kerns at the mean of his two
   answers.
2. **"Show me options first"** for a uniform loosening. His re-asked answers ran
   +5.4 units looser than his 09-21 answers on typical rows. This part is
   options only. The defaults do not change.

## 1. What a bench delta means [verified]

- **Unit.** The slider runs −60..60 "thousandths of an em". Both bench fonts
  have `unitsPerEm` 1000, so one step is one font unit.
- **Zero.** The page splits the word after the judged pair's left letter. It
  puts the tail in a span with `margin-left = (k + d) / 1000 em`, where `k` is
  the item's `kr`/`ki` (`bench_reask.py` page template, `paint()`). The fonts are
  `bench/fonts-2026-09-20/`. I shaped each pair in those fonts with HarfBuzz and
  checked that `k` equals the GPOS kern they carried. It does for all six:
  Fi 0, Fo −36, Ye −108, Yo −108, `'s` −18, `'t` 0. So zero is the pair **as it
  shipped on 2026-09-20**, and `d` is the change to that pair's white, in units.
  One thing is assumed and not verified: the browser does not shape GPOS across
  the span boundary. This is the page's stated design and the same one the
  original bench used.
- **Relative, not absolute.** Target white = (the pair's white in the
  09-20 fonts) + (the mean of his two `d`). It is **not** "current + mean",
  because the letters' bearings have moved since 09-20.
- **Which apostrophe.** The bench words are ASCII (`Suvi's`, `don't`), so the
  glyph he judged was **quotesingle** (U+0027). His books set U+2019:
  `pair_census.py` folds `’` to `'` (line 24), so the 5,782 `'s` in his books
  are mostly **quoteright**. Both glyphs measure the same white in the 09-20
  fonts and in the current build (rsb 81 on both), so both carry the kern. This
  follows the BENCH_DELTAS precedent in `kern.py` ("both apostrophes carry").

White is `rsb(L) + kern + lsb(R)`, with the kern read from the GPOS by
HarfBuzz. The instrument is
`tools/wedge_serif/instruments/r388_pair_white.py`.

## 5. Tracking options: `ALBO_TRACK=a|b|c` (defaults unchanged)

The owner ruled "show me options first". Code: `outlines/build.py`,
`TRACK_ARMS` / `_track_side`, applied right after `fit()`. It changes
sidebearings only, split evenly between lsb and rsb, in both styles and all four
cuts. Kerns are untouched, since no kern reads the variable.

**How the sides are derived.** Each arm is stated as what a bench PAIR gains.
- A lowercase pair is rsb + lsb, so a lowercase letter takes T/2 on each side.
- A mark pair is a lowercase letter's side plus a mark's side, so marks also
  take T/2 on each side.
- A bench capital pair is a capital followed by a lowercase letter (every cap
  row on the bench: Fi Ye Qu To ...). The lowercase letter's left half already
  delivers the capital target, so **the capitals themselves do not move**.
- Figures, fences and symbols do not move, and neither does the Greek. None of
  them was judged. The word space does not move either.
- Accented Latin lowercase letters and the roman f-ligatures follow their base.

| arm | per side, lowercase and marks | lowercase pair | mark pair | cap + lowercase pair | capital + capital |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **a** (unset) | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| **b** | 1.5 | +3 | +3 (measured +2..+4) | +1.5 (measured +1..+2) | 0 |
| **c** | 3 | +6 | +6 | +3 | 0 |

Arm b's 1.5-unit sides round to integers per glyph, so each pair lands within
±1 of its target. That is under the phone's 1.16-unit quantum.

**Measured effect** (400s, RAQM/GPOS):

| | roman b | roman c | italic b | italic c |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `minimum` / `rhythm` / `Yesterday` (units) | +21 / +18 / +24 | +42 / +36 / +48 | same | same |
| Austen paragraph (482 chars), set length | +0.54% | +1.08% | +0.67% | +1.34% |
| lines at 54 px em in a 672 px column (a → arm) | 19 → 19 | 19 → 20 | 15 → 16 | 15 → 16 |
| lines at 20 px em in a 338 px column (a → arm) | 14 → 14 | 14 → 15 | 11 → 11 | 11 → 11 |

The italic moves more in percentage terms because its letters are narrower.

**Default proved unchanged.** A build with `ALBO_TRACK` unset was compared with
the committed round-388 build using `cmp_outlines.py --advances`: all four cuts
**IDENTICAL** (0 of 530 outlines, advances included). `gates.sh`: `GATES
UNCHANGED`.

**Proofs.** 13 pt on the 2x app is a 54 px em (`kern.py`'s header). That is 27
px on the X3 at 1x. There is one image per arm, `track-{a,b,c}-paragraph.png`:
the Austen paragraph in roman and then italic, at 54 px 1:1 and at 20 px shown
2× nearest. Lines wrap greedily at the same column in every arm, so a changed
line count is part of the effect. There is also one word strip per arm,
`track-{a,b,c}-words.png` ("minimum rhythm the quick brown fox; don't
Yesterday"). All images are lossless PNGs, 700 px wide, in session scratch
`r388/`.

**Awaiting the owner's pick.** A winning arm ships by changing the `"a"`
default in `ALBO_TRACK`. It will then fail `gates.sh`'s `bench_fit --check`
ledger only if it touches build.py's bearing tables, which it does not. It will
move every lowercase advance, and the approved-glyph check compares outlines,
not advances.
