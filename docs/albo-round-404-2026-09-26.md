# Albo round 404: the italic straight apostrophe as a pen wedge (2026-09-26)

Owner ruling, 2026-09-26, on the variations of b: **"j wins"**. j is the pen
wedge with its top cut at 28°, the italic's finial face.

The options and their measurements are in `docs/albo-apostrophe-2026-09-26.md`,
§10–11.

## 1. What changed

| dial (`outlines/glyphs/marks.py`) | before | round 404 |
|---|---|---|
| `ALBO_APOS_IT_STRAIGHT` | `a` (a parallel stroke, 1.42 stems wide) | **`j`** in Italic and BoldItalic. The roman never reads the dial |

The mark j draws:

- the italic `'` is drawn upright and takes the italic's shear;
- it tapers from 1.20 stems to a 0.25 point over today's length;
- its top is cut at `PR.FINIAL_CUT_DEG` (28°), not the family's 20°.

Measured, Italic 400:

| | before (round 403) | round 404 |
|---|---|---|
| height, as a fraction of the x-height | 0.531 | 0.561 |
| width at top / middle / foot, in stems | 1.42 / 1.42 / 1.41 | 0.85 / 0.80 / 0.32 |
| ink, as a fraction of an n's | 0.327 | 0.172 |

The italic references' ink runs 0.159–0.192.

`spacing_b2.json`, `kern.py` and `figures.py` were not touched. The bearings
are taken off the mark's ink, so the horizontal white beside it is held. The
advance of `quotesingle` shrinks:

- Italic: 166 → 123;
- BoldItalic: 225 → 152.
