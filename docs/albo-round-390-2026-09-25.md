## The arithmetic

Zero on the page is the pair's white as round 389 ships it (the page embeds
`bench/fonts-2026-09-25/`, the `49deaff` build). A delta is a change to that
white in font units (1000 upm), + looser. `bench_outliers.py kerns` computes
`add = (page white + his delta) - white in the build under test`.

The HEAD build (`59836cc`) was checked outline-identical to the page's fonts
(`cmp_outlines.py`: 0 of 530 glyphs differ, both styles) and every pair's
white matched the page, so **add = his delta exactly** for all 50 rows. Each is
written as `PAIRS[p] = _shipped(*p) + add` (round 388/389 form), in
`outlines/kern.py` under `ROUND 390`, italic-gated on `_ALD.ON`; the bold cuts
inherit their style's value.

**Kern quantum.** The phone's quantum is 1.16 units, so every integer answer
survives; no value was rounded.
