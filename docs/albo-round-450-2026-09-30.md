# Albo round 450: the accents resized to fit, and centered (2026-09-30)

Owner, 2026-09-30, during round 449: ***"resize accents to fit rest and center the marks. show me proof"***.

Proof page: https://claude.ai/artifact/CyfActYFJ2duvzxVwauFos.

Rounds 449 (every accented letter kerns as its base), 450 and 451 ship together. **Round 451 (`docs/albo-round-451-2026-09-30.md`) supersedes three parts of this record:** the tilde (an even wave, lighter in the bolds), the ogonek (a hook hung by its attach point), and the `clearance_composite` block (regenerated: 443 kerns). It also mitered every chevron (circumflex, caron, guillemets, < > ≤ ≥ ^). 450 changes the marks and fixes a placement bug, so 449's collision kerns were regenerated on the new marks; they are NOT the 111 that `docs/albo-round-449-2026-09-30.md` lists.

## How it was measured

`tools/wedge_serif/instruments/acc_fit.py` (new) reads every accent off its OWN LETTER: the accented glyph's raster minus its base's, unhinted, 1 px per unit. It measures, per the font's x-height:
- the mark's width and height;
- its mean stroke width over the font's stem;
- its gap over the letter;
- its centering, three ways: on the letter's ink box, on its top half, and on a stem letter's stem. In an italic every center is carried along the slant.

References, each style against its own: Pagella, Flanker, Georgia, ITC Berkeley Oldstyle and Times New Roman, plus Coelacanth in the italic. `instruments/acc_sheet.py` (new) draws the before/after sheets.

**THE BUG (found here and, independently, by round 449's review).**
- **The cause:** a composite's left sidebearing was its BASE's ink edge. TrueType rasterisers place an outline so that its xMin lands on hmtx's lsb (phantom point pp1 = xMin − lsb). So every accented letter whose mark reaches further left than the letter was drawn shifted right by the difference, letter and mark together. The amounts:

  | Cut | Shifted right |
  |---|---|
  | Regular | ï +44, Ï +43, ĭ +15, ì +11, î +9, ĩ +6 |
  | Bold | ï +113, Ï +109, ä +40 |
  | Bold Italic | ï +48 |
  | All four | the combining marks |

- **The reader shows it.** Its font converter rasterises with FreeType and stores `bitmap_left` (`fontconvert_sdcard.py`).
- **Why nobody saw it:** shaped ADVANCES and KERNS do not depend on lsb, so every shaped measurement looked right. (First written as "HarfBuzz ignores lsb", which is wrong: uharfbuzz draws outlines with the same shift. Corrected by the round-450 review.)
- **It is round 449's "Bold ï touches everything".** The 288 Bold contacts were the shift.

**The accent collision sweep, extended** (`cmp_touch.py --composites`, round 449):
- **More right-hand glyphs:** the curly quotes, guillemets, ellipsis and dashes. Round 449's review found a Bold Italic ö” touching that ASCII `"` did not show.
- **2-D measure:** where the row-wise white is under 0.05 em, the 2-D closest approach is taken from edge pixels. A mark meets a W's or Y's shoulder diagonally, and the review found Bold Italic Wä 7 units apart while its row-wise white read exactly the floor. kern.py's Q and q kerns were sized on the same 2-D distance (rounds 348 and 357).

**`local_ai/clearance.py --composites` no longer caps at the unkerned position** (`--cap` restores it):
- **Why the cap existed:** the shifted letters made "unkerned" contacts the bug's, and kerns would have papered over it.
- **Why it can go:** with the bug fixed, an accent that meets its neighbor with no kern is the mark's own contact. It gets the 0.015 em the base clearance gives a base pair. The case that decided it is the italic í before Albo's ascender-high ? ("¿Aquí?"), which the larger acute, set right, now reaches.

**`spacing_b2.json`:**
- **Size:** 390 `clearance_composite` kerns (Regular 69, Italic 131, Bold 60, Bold Italic 130), regenerated from empty on the final marks.
- **Convergence:** clearance passes until the last pass added none, except one italic non-word, ĭV, which the 2-D measure holds at 0.0115 em against the 0.012 floor.
- **Size of the fixes:** most are small. The large ones sit on an accented i next to a quote, ? or another accented i, where the full-width mark overhangs the i's narrow advance; the references draw the same full-width marks on the i (only Georgia narrows them).
- **The Western pairs that occur in text:** italic í? +25, í' +9, í‘ +18; Bold Italic í? +11.

## Verified

- **What moved:** only marks (26 a cut: spacing and combining) and accented letters (165 Regular / Bold, 169 Italic / Bold Italic). No other glyph's outline or advance moved. Advances moved only for the spacing marks themselves.
- **Left sidebearings:** every one in all four cuts now equals its glyph's xMin (0 mismatches; 13–24 a cut before).
- **Base pairs:** 0 kerning differences in all four cuts against round 448 (HarfBuzz, every pair of the base glyphs).
- **Accented pairs:** each equals its base pair except the `clearance_composite` pairs.
- **Accent faults** (`cmp_touch.py --composites`): 0 touching in every cut; 1 under the floor (italic ĭV, above).
  - On the same sweep, round 448 had Regular 28, Italic 334, Bold 329 and Bold Italic 507.
  - Inherited kerns alone, before any clearance, had Regular 69, Italic 131, Bold 60 and Bold Italic 130.
- **Default touch gate:** 0 / 0 in every cut.
- **poor_gates against 448:** no delta. hairs new [] cleared []; touch 0 → 0; glitch counts unchanged (an accent is its own island); contour census unchanged (1056 glyphs); approved 2/2; e mouth ok.
- **gates.sh:** GATES UNCHANGED.
- **The device's kerning budget** at 10 pt: classes up to 87 × 92 (Bold Italic), a matrix of 8,004 B against `MEASURE_KERN_ARENA_LIMIT` 16 KB.

## Negative results and notes

- **acc_fit.py's first gap column read the mark's TOP, not its bottom**, because raster row indices run downward. It was caught when Albo read 0.31 x-heights of gap against its own constant of 0.10, and fixed before any number here.
- **The i takes the full-width marks.** Pagella, Times, Flanker, Coelacanth and Berkeley draw the same mark width on the i as on the o; only Georgia narrows it, by about 0.1 x-height. Narrower i-marks would mean separate mark glyphs. That was not asked for, and the collision kerns cover the contacts.
- **The ogonek offset in stems overshot the bold** (+0.04 against +0.19 at 1.45 stems), because the bold's stem is 1.7× the 400's. It is in x-heights now.
- **Two of the seven italic marks still differ from the references' centering**, and were left: the italic circumflex reads 0 against −0.04 and the caron 0 against +0.06. The references disagree among themselves by more than that (−0.11 to +0.19).
