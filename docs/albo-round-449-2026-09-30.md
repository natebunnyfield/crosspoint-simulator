# Albo round 449: every accented letter kerns as its base does (2026-09-30)

> **Read with round 450** (`docs/albo-round-450-2026-09-30.md`). The two shipped together. The 111 collision kerns below were sized on accented letters that a sidebearing bug drew shifted right, so round 450 fixed the bug and regenerated them (390) on its resized marks. The leftover-contact diagnosis at the foot was wrong for the same reason; see *Corrections*.

Owner, 2026-09-30, asked whether to fix the gap round 448's adversarial review opened up: ***"Fix all accented (Recommended)"***. The option read: every accented letter gets its base letter's kerning, in all four cuts, then the collision sweep, so a tight accent cannot touch its neighbor.

## The defect (measured on round 448's shipped fonts)

**The cause:** `kern.py`'s classes (`LEFT` / `RIGHT`) and exact `PAIRS` name BASE glyphs only. An accented composite takes its base's sidebearings (`build.py` fits it "so the accented composites follow their base"), but it took none of its base's kerning. `_extend_holds` (round 442) passed the r's `rsnip` deltas on and nothing else.

**Kerned base pairs whose accented form differed by 10 units or more** (distinct base pairs; an earlier version of this table counted every accented-letter-and-neighbor combination, 2,010 of 2,632 in the Regular, and its Bold row repeated the Regular's):

| Cut | Differing | Kerned base pairs |
|---|---|---|
| Regular | 703 | 1,183 |
| Italic | 787 | 1,333 |
| Bold | 723 | 1,207 |
| Bold Italic | 793 | 1,349 |

- **Worst:** `WA` −216 against `WÁ` 0.
- **In the owner's own 41 epubs** (2,812,153 adjacent character pairs; 2,568,234 letter-to-letter), 5,132 occurrences in the Regular and 6,050 in the Italic, mostly Spanish (the tico-spanish books): `ía`, `ué`, `rá`, `é,`, `í.`, `rí`, `és`. `pair_census.py` cannot see accented letters: its word regex is ASCII-only.

## What changed

**`outlines/kern.py`** (drafted by a subagent to this brief, reviewed and extended here):
- **`_composite_family(font)`:** every encoded glyph whose NFD starts with a DIFFERENT letter the font also encodes. í is i's; ≠ is nobody's.
- **`_extend_composites(font)`, run in `apply()`:**
  - **Classes:** each composite joins its base's class on each side, unless that side already names it. It never joins a second class on one side: feaLib does not refuse that, it opens a second subtable, and HarfBuzz stops at the first subtable that covers the first glyph.
  - **Exact pairs:** every exact pair's FINAL value goes to every composite combination not already set. An explicit composite pair is kept. "Final" means after the B2 kerns, holds, fences, clearance and `rsnip`.
  - **`rsnip`:** the r's deltas are counted once, since they are already in the base pair's value. `RSNIP_HOLDS` is gone. The Italic `ř.` goes −8 → −90, equal to `r.`.
  - **Collision fixes:** the new `clearance_composite` block is applied at the END of `_extend_composites`. Each value is therefore a delta on what the composite inherited.

**`cmp_touch.py --composites`** (new mode; the default gate is untouched):
- **Scope:** sweeps every pair with an accented letter on either side, measured exactly as the default sweep measures (row-wise white after kerning, 0.012 em floor).
- **What it reports:** only pairs where the ACCENT is the fault. That means the pair is under the floor while its base pair clears. Where the base pair is itself under the floor (the exempt f+ascender and Q pairs, scripts the default sweep skips), the accent must make it worse by more than 0.002 em. A base pair that ligates excuses nothing: `fí` does not ligate.
- **Skipped:**
  - **The roman Q's tail against a mark below** (Qç, Qļ, Qą). By the round-225 ruling that tail pairs only with capitals and u, and these pairs occur in no language.
  - **Pairs across scripts**, e.g. a Greek letter then an Esperanto ĵ.
- **Each fault line** also prints the pair's own kern, and "unkerned" where the pair is under the floor even with no kern.

**`local_ai/clearance.py --composites`** (new mode) writes the `clearance_composite` block of `spacing_b2.json`, per cut:
- **The amount:** the kern that lifts the pair to 0.015 em, the same target as the base clearance.
- **The cap:** a fix may only undo the inherited kern, never open a pair past its unkerned position. What the inherited kern closed is undone. What the drawing closes stays the drawing's problem (below).

**`spacing_b2.json`:** 111 `clearance_composite` kerns (Regular 20, Italic 4, Bold 48, Bold Italic 39).
- T Ţ Ť Ț + ä ë ö ÿ š in the bolds (+26 to +61)
- Y Ý Ŷ Ÿ + ä ë ÿ
- F and V + ä ë
- Ï and Ĭ + A and its accented forms in the roman (the `IA` −72 kern the Ï inherited)
- In the Italic, only T + š
- Plus a few against punctuation (`ï'`, `ë?`).

## Left to the drawing (recorded, not fixed)

These accented pairs meet with no kern at all, so no kern of this round made them. 16 / 13 / 288 / 166 of them remain:
- **Most of the Bold's 288 and the Bold Italic's 166 are ï and Ï.** The bold dieresis is wider than its i and reaches ascenders, capitals and quotes on either side (ïl, Ïb, ïT, ï').
- **The rest:**
  - ļ and į against a following g (the italic g's descender curls back under them);
  - ĵ's circumflex;
  - a few Greek pairs.
- **The fix is in the drawing**, a narrower bold dieresis on the i, not a kern on every pair that touches it. This is a proposal, not done.

## Traps (for whoever touches this next)

- **Composite clearance goes in its own block.** Written into the old `clearance` block, a composite pair's kern is applied BEFORE the composites exist. There `_shipped` returns 0 for it, so it lands as a bare delta, and `_extend_composites` then keeps it as an explicit ruling. The `clearance_composite` block, applied after the inheritance, exists for this reason.
- **An explicit fix reaches only its exact pair.** That is why the composite sweep measures Ť, Ţ and Ț separately from T. They got their own entries.
- **`kern.py`'s comment near line 74 is inaccurate** (the subagent's finding and measurement, fontTools 4.64 / uharfbuzz 0.56; not re-run here, and the comment is not edited). It says feaLib REFUSES a glyph in two classes of one PairPos. It does not: it opens a second class subtable, which HarfBuzz never reaches for a first glyph the first subtable covers. The one-class-per-side rule is still right; the stated reason is wrong.

## Corrections (adversarial review of round 449, and round 450)

- **The leftover contacts were a placement bug, not the drawing.** *Left to the drawing* above says the Bold's ï and Ï contacts come from a dieresis wider than its i. They do not. A composite's left sidebearing was its BASE's ink edge, and FreeType places an outline so its xMin lands on that lsb. So every accented letter whose mark reaches further left than the letter was drawn shifted right: the Bold ï by 113 units, Ï by 109, ä by 40; the Regular ï by 44. In all 286 of the Bold's ï/Ï contacts the ï/Ï comes first; "on either side" was wrong. Round 450 fixed the sidebearings, and the Bold's accent contacts fell 288 → 78 on that alone.
- **The 111 collision kerns were sized on the shifted letters.** With the bug fixed, Bold Tä and Yä collide and the Ï+A kerns run loose. Round 450 regenerated the whole `clearance_composite` block on its resized marks (390 kerns), with curly quotes and a 2-D closest approach added to the sweep.
- **The sweep missed curly quotes.** It tried only ASCII `'` and `"`, and Bold Italic ö” (an inherited −18) touched unseen. Fixed in round 450's sweep.
- **Smaller numbers:** the T family's kerns run +4 to +61, not +26 to +61; Bold Italic T + ï +10 was missing from the list; no Greek pair remains (*a few Greek pairs* was wrong); the base sweep's 334 glyphs are the letters, figures, punctuation and symbols (342 counting every non-composite, non-mark glyph; the review swept all 342 with the same result).
