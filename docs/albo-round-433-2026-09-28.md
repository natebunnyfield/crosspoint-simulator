**Why the finial floor was not enough:** `C_FIN_BOT_700` below about 0.6 changes nothing. The finial's own swell (1.10 × the end's width) then governs the bottom, not the floor.

**New dial:** `ALBO_ALD_C_TAIL[_700]` tapers the widths over the last `C_TAIL_T` (0.25) of the path, 1.0 = off. The 700 also takes its own `C_FIN_TOP_700` / `C_FIN_BOT_700`. At default the build is identical to round 433.
