### The top, second pass (owner: *"t3 but needs top read as a c better (the top sucks)"*)

**What was wrong:** in T3 the top finial ran up-right as a spike above the x-height (ink top 507). In every reference (Flanker, Georgia, Berkeley, Pagella) the crown arches over and the terminal hangs down the right side.

**What was tried:**
- **Rejected: growing the swell inward only**, shifting the centerline along the inward normal by half the added width. It self-intersected into a hole and flipped the terminal face across the counter. Removed, not left as a dial.
- **Rejected: a longer swell span** (0.13 → 0.22 / 0.32). No visible change to the shoulder step.
- **What works: the start angle.** `C_A0_700`, parametric, down from 50°:
  - 25–35° still ends at the squarish ellipse's top-right corner, as a horizontal beak.
  - 15° hangs the terminal.
  - It widens the c: advance 350 → 397, c/o ink width 0.78 → 0.94.
  - `C_W_700` brings the width back. The references' c/o is Flanker 0.97, Pagella 0.89, Berkeley 0.88, Georgia 0.87.
- **Taper fix:** `C_TAIL_T` (0.25) started the bottom taper before the bowl's lowest point, thinning the bottom and losing the overshoot (−8 → −1). At 0.12 the taper starts past the bottom, and the overshoot is back at −8.

### The top, third pass: DRAWN (owner on X1/X3: *"much worse and worse, do better"*)

**The diagnosis, from Poetica, Coelacanth, Cancelleresca, Flanker, Pagella, Times and Berkeley at one x-height:**
- Every reference c has a ROUND CROWN that arches over. The terminal hangs off its right end at about 0.75–0.85 xh, and its underside hooks back into the counter.
- Albo's c has no arch, in both the 400 (F2) and the 700 (T3). The crown is a straight ramp rising right, and the terminal is the highest point of the letter: a horn.
- The swelled finial grows width about the path, so it cannot make a hanging lobe. X1/X3 turned that into a flag.

**The construction** (`_c_drawn_top`, `ALBO_ALD_C_TOPDRAW_700=1`; off by default, and a true default build is byte-identical to round 433):
- The stroke stops at a junction on the crown (`C_TJ`).
- A terminal is unioned on:
  - an outer cubic continuing the crown's arch down to the tip (`C_TX`, `C_TY`);
  - a straight cut face (`C_TF` long at `C_TFA` degrees) — the family's wedge, not a ball;
  - one concave underside back to the crown's inner edge.
- Both drawn edges leave along the stroke's OWN edges at the junction.

**Findings while tuning, each seen at 4× with the points marked:**
- A junction right of the apex (80–86°) leaves a corner on the crown. At 100° the arch is unbroken.
- The underside must leave the face steeply (120–125°) with a short arrival handle. Otherwise its second control point sits right of its first, the curve swings left–right–left, and the counter's top-right becomes a slot.
- Overlays against Poetica (unsheared 11°) and Coelacanth (whose font declares no italic angle; unsheared 12° by hand) confirmed the terminal's position. Tool: scratchpad `cqov.py`.
