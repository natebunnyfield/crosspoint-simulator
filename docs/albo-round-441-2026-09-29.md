### Pen edge and metal punch: the owner's picks D1, F2, B20b

Owner, on option 18: *"no round lobe, pen edge and metal punch like rest"*. Keep the thickness; build it the way Albo's other thick terminals are built.

**What "like rest" means, looked at first.** Albo's own thick terminals (the c's top, the s, the a's exit) are all a pen curve that ends in a flat, slanted face with crisp corners. Measured by the same instrument:
- the c's top: 163‰ (Italic), 175‰ (Bold Italic);
- the s: 137‰ / 235‰;
- round 441's r: already 189‰ / 213‰.

**Negative results. Do not re-propose these:**
- **Faceted top edge** (straight out of the arm's own top edge, then a corner; tried as `R_TPOLY`, withdrawn). At the junction the arm's top edge is still climbing steeply, so any straight run out of it overshoots the x-line into a spike: tops 475–499 against the o's 444 (ladder P1–P3).
- **441's snipped arm hung lower** (`R_SX/R_SY/R_SEA` steeper, ladder H1–H3). Three problems:
  - The width keys are FRACTIONS of the path length, so a longer path moves the root's thickening outward, leaving a tooth where the arm leaves the stem.
  - Without `R_ENDHULL` the widening end curls into the hook ruled out in round 439.
  - With it, the underside becomes one long straight edge (a triangular flag).
- **A round inner arch under the terminal** (`R_TUB` 0.3–0.45 at `R_TUA` 95–112, ladder E1–E4). The terminal thins toward its bottom, which reads as a claw or beak, and drops to 153–178‰.

**What works: the drawn terminal (`R_TERM`, the code of options 14–18) with a straight underside and a filed bottom corner (`R_TCH` 1.5).** The top edge is the pen's curve over the crest. The face is flat. The underside is straight back to the arm, meeting it in a crisp V, as Albo's wedge serifs meet their strokes. No code change was needed: every setting below is an existing dial, and `aldine.py` is unchanged.

**The owner picked three from the unsheared overlay ladders:** *"B20b F2 D1 are best"*. Each was then drawn in both italics with the same values (the `_700` twins):
