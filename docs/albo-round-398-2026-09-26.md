## 3. The Bold Italic g

**Owner 2026-09-26:** *"check that 'g' is right in bold italic"*.

### Cause [verified in source]

- **Round 269** put every absolute width in `glyphs/aldine.py` on the weight
  axis through `ALD_WF_UP = max(1, S / 84)` (`aldine.py:194`). For the g, it
  did so in **`_g_roman`**, whose rings take `wscale=ALD_WF_UP * G_WALL`
  (`aldine.py:5486`, `:5499`). That was the g that shipped then.
- **The cursive arm** (`a_g`, `ALBO_ALD_G_STYLE=cursive`, the default since)
  never got the factor. Its bowl and loop call `keyed_ring(..., pen=(G_BOWL_PEN
  …))` and `pen=(G_LOOP_PEN …)` **with no `wscale`**, so it defaults to 1.0.
  Its neck widths (`G_NECK_W`, `G_NECK_TWIST`, `_startw`, `_endw`), its ear
  (`G_EAR_T`) and its oval guard (`G_OVAL_WALL`) are all in absolute units.
- **So the 700 drew the 400's g stroke for stroke.**

### Fix

- `_G_WF` (`ALBO_ALD_G_WF`, `aldine.py:5332`) multiplies every **width** of
  the cursive g: both rings via `wscale`, the neck, the ear and the oval
  guard. No position moves.
- It applies only above the Medium (`S > 84`). **At the 400 it is exactly
  1.0**, so `approved.py --check` passes the approved Italic g.
- **Its value, 1.65, is measured.** It is the Bold Italic bowl family's median
  chamfer stroke over the Italic's: 97.1 / 58.7 = 1.654. ALD_WF_UP alone
  (1.381) leaves the g light, because the 700's family is heavier than that
  factor (round 274's junctions, the stems' ALD_WF).

### Weight [measured, `cmp_weight_survey.py`; units at 380 px]

Family = a o q d b p (median).

| | g thin | g stroke | g thick | g colour | family thin / stroke / thick / colour | g / family (thin, stroke, thick, colour) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Italic 400 (approved) | 27.1 | 49.7 | 61.7 | 0.273 | 36.9 / 58.7 / 73.4 / 0.300 | .73 .85 .84 .91 |
| Bold Italic, before | 26.3 | 47.4 | 60.2 | 0.271 | 48.9 / 97.1 / 106.9 / 0.412 | .54 .49 .56 .66 |

- **Ladder** (g / family): ALD_WF_UP 1.381 → .77 .67 .74 .83; 1.50 → .80 .71
  .80 .87; **1.65 → .89 .77 .85 .93**.
- **1.65 matches the Italic's own thick and colour ratios.** Its stroke (74.5)
  sits inside the Bold Italic lowercase's own range (49.7 to 99.3), beside
  `e` 70, `o` 74 and `z` 74.5.
- **What moved:** the Bold Italic `g gbreve gcircumflex gdotaccent uni0123`
  outlines, nothing else. Advances and GPOS are unchanged. Every other cut is
  identical.
