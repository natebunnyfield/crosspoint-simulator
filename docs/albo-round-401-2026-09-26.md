# Albo round 401: rendered with no hinting (2026-09-26)

Owner ruling, 2026-09-26, after the hinting proof page: **"no hinting wins"**.
The measurement it was made on: `docs/albo-hinting-options-2026-09-26.md`.

**A rendering change only. No outline, advance, kern, ligature or metric moved.**
The fonts are round 400's (`9e28bb5`), unchanged.

## 1. What changed

| where | before | round 401 |
|---|---|---|
| `crosspoint-reader` `lib/EpdFont/scripts/sd-fonts.yaml`, Albo | no hinting key (FreeType default = the autohinter, NORMAL target, since Albo carries no bytecode) | **`hinting: none`** |
| `fontconvert_sdcard.py` | `FT_LOAD_RENDER` for every face | the family's own faces `FT_LOAD_RENDER \| FT_LOAD_NO_HINTING`; fallback faces unchanged |
| `build-sd-fonts.py` | — | `hinting: none` → `--no-hinting`; any other value, or with `force_autohint`, refuses to build |
| `tools/wedge_serif/etrace/e_hint_gate.py` (in `gates.sh`) | the e's mouth on the AUTOHINTED render, 8–12 ppem | the e's mouth UNHINTED, as the converter renders it: every reader slot (1x + 2x, 8-bit + 2-bit) ≤ 0.15, 9–12 ppem ≤ 0.20; `--autohint` keeps the old check |

Firmware commit `db8a36a4b` (local, not pushed).

## 2. What it does on the device (measured, round 399/400 fonts)

- **Weight:** the roman at 1x drew 4–10% heavier than its outline under the
  autohinter (stems 9–27% wider from 10 pt up); unhinted it draws at 0.97–1.01
  of the outline, Italic 0.97–1.00, Bold and BoldItalic 0.99–1.00. **Every
  earlier judgment of the roman's weight on a device was made on the heavier
  rendering.**
- **Stems:** the same lowercase stem is now drawn 6–7 ways across the letters
  rather than 2–3 (each glyph's stem lands at its own sub-pixel phase).
- **Edges:** x-height and baseline edges of round letters are greyer (edge
  crispness 0.67 against 0.87).
- **Reading:** Apple Vision on the converter's own pixels, every reader size,
  1x and 2x, all four cuts: 99.5–100%, 0 e>o — the same as before.
- **The e:** open at every reader size (mouth_block 0.00). Index e>o 1
  (round 400 autohinted: 3). One residual below every reader size: at exactly
  8 px an unhinted e reads as o 13 times in 84 (Georgia 0); 8.5 px 1, 9 px up
  0. Not an outline change for the reader; recorded as an option for the
  owner in the hinting doc §8.
- **Bytes:** Albo's .cpfont files grow 0.15–0.54% (full `reading` build with the fallback chain).

## 3. Proof it is exactly the measured arm, and nothing else moved

- **Other families byte-identical:** Edgar and TeXGyreHeros (the latter with
  `force_autohint`, the other load-flag path) built before and after the
  converter change: 12 of 12 .cpfont identical.
- **Albo = the measured NO HINTING arm:** the new build's 1x files are
  byte-identical (6/6) to the same recipe run through the scratch-patched
  converter that produced the hinting doc's numbers.
- **Seed tree:** `build-sd-fonts.py --only Albo` at 1x and `--scale 2` into
  `crosspoint-reader/fs_/fonts`; every one of the twelve files changed md5;
  `tools/validate_seed_fonts.py fs_/fonts --recipe sd-fonts.yaml`: **OK, 13
  families**. `Albo/3x` untouched (above the bundling ceiling, not checked).
- **Gate:** round 400 passes unhinted (reader 0.000; 9–12 worst 0.149 at 9.0);
  a bar dropped to 0.52 x-height FAILS (0.345), so the gate still bites.
