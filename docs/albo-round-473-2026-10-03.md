# Albo round 473: fixes from the adversarial review of rounds 465–472 (2026-10-03)

Rounds 465–472 shipped without an adversarial review: the standing phase
(`~/.claude/skills/adversarial-review`) was skipped. One was run after the
fact: a read-only agent covering the diffs of 8c425ac … f7b86c1 and the built
fonts of rounds 464 and 472. It reported five findings. Each was reproduced
here before it was fixed, and this round ships the fixes.

**No outline moves in this round.** Every change is a kern or a font metric.

## The findings, and what was done

**1. The italic x's fence kerns were stale after round 472**
(silent-wrong-output; MY miss).
- **Cause.** Round 472 cut the x's bearings (lsb −20, rsb −6 / −12) and re-ran
  the clearance loop, but not `local_ai/fences.py`.
- **Reproduced** (`fence_gap` band gap, close − open):

  | | (x) | [x] | {x} |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Italic, round 471 | −1 | −1 | +1 |
  | Italic, round 472 | +13 | +13 | +15 |
  | Bold Italic, round 472 | +9 | +8 | +8 |

  The shift equals the bearing change.
- **Fixed:** `fences.py --cuts Italic,BoldItalic` on an `ALBO_FENCES=0` build.
  The x's fence kerns go from ±22/26/28 to ±16/20/20 (Italic) and from
  ±18/25/25 to ±14/21/21 (Bold Italic). Round 473 measures +1 / +1 / −1 and
  +1 / 0 / 0.
- **Lesson:** any round that moves a letter's bearings re-runs `fences.py`
  for that cut, as rounds 466 and 468 did.

**2. Every fence now reaches below the declared descent** (latent).
- **The reach.** Round 468's fences go to −339 / −335 / −358 / −344 (R / I / B
  / Z). That is past `usWinDescent` 320 in all four cuts, and past the
  reader's declared descent −343 (`sd-fonts.yaml`) by 15 units in the Bold
  and 1 in the Bold Italic.
- **Fixed, the font metric:** `VM_WIN_DESCENT` 320 → 360 (`build.py`), the
  same fix round 100 made on the ascent side. Windows clips ink past it; the
  reader does not read it.
- **NOT changed, the reader's declared descent:** that changes the reader's
  layout, so it is a question for the owner, recorded below.

**3. The Regular Q( kern no longer cleared** (cosmetic, rare pair).
- **Cause.** `kern.py`'s hand Q kerns were measured to leave 0.012 em. Round
  468's taller "(" brought the Regular Q's tail to 1.3 units from it (it had
  been 19.6). `cmp_touch` exempts the pair, so no gate could see it.
- **Fixed:** `_Q_KERNS['(']` 440 → 452. The Regular Q( measures 0.016 em, the
  Bold 0.034.

**4. The touch gate could not see [ ] { } | ¦** (cosmetic).
- **Cause.** `cmp_touch.py` swept only `.,;:!?'"()-`, and `clearance.py` uses
  it. So round 468's "touch 0 / 0" covered only the parentheses.
- **Fixed:** the sweep includes them (about 6,200 pairs per cut, from 5,309).
  On round 472 it finds 9 touching pairs in the Regular and 6 in the Bold. The
  clearance loop then added:
  - **]j, |j, ¦j:** +20, +21 and +21 (Regular). The j's tail tip runs inside
    the longer stems.
  - **Q[ Q] Q{ Q} Q| Q¦:** +360 to +487 (Regular and Bold). Albo's long Q tail
    runs under any tall fence. These are the same "expensive" kind as the hand
    Q( / Q) kerns (round 225's argument: sequences that do not occur in
    English).

  Round 473 measures touch 0 / 0 in every cut.

**5. Round 470 left composite clearance kerns sized for the old accent dots**
(cosmetic, rare pairs).
- **Cause.** `clearance.py` only ever adds. After round 470 shrank the
  Regular's dieresis and dot-above, 28 of its kerns (Ï Ï +19, Ī Ï +30,
  Ø Ï +75, …) held pairs 13–39 units apart where 12 is the floor.
- **Fixed:** all 28 removed. The composite pass re-added only what the new
  dots need (16 kerns over five passes). Regular composites measure 0
  touching and 0 under the floor across 63,772 accented pairs.

## What the review checked and found CLEAN (recorded so the next pass does not redo it)

- **No fence is built outside `build.draw`.**
  - The only callers of `paren` / `bracket` / the brace builders / `g_bar` /
    `g_brokenbar`, and the only readers of `FENCE_RAISE`, are the eight fences.
  - The font has no ⁽ ⁾ ₍ ₎ ⸨ ❨ ⟨ ⟩, and © ® ™ do not call them.
- **No threads anywhere in `outlines/`** (`variable.py` runs one subprocess per
  master). `fence_lines()` runs before `PR.begin_glyph`, both fence draws call
  it, and `glyphs.CURRENT` is saved and restored.
- **The fences land on their lines:** all 8 × 4 cuts within ±1 unit of
  max(b d h k l) + 40 and min(g j p q y) − 40. No collateral glyph change
  between rounds 464 and 472.
- **The broken bar's gap** is centered on `MID` (214.5) in every cut.
- **Tittles:** every outline change from round 464 to 472 maps to a listed
  commit. The italics' i, j, ĳ, ﬁ, ﬃ and U+E002 are unchanged.
  - `dot_y` uses `TIT_RI` where the i and j draw `TIT_RI_ADJ`, which moves the
    dot's floor by −0.3 units (Regular) and −0.6 (Bold): negligible, measured.
- **Dot accents:** all 25 Regular glyphs have dots of 81.8–82.0 units, against
  the i's 81.6 and the j's 82.0.
  - The Bold and italic accent glyphs are identical to round 464.
  - The j's dot moved with the i, and İ moved. The font has no U+0323 or ŀ.
- **S width:**
  - S, $, § and Ś Ŝ Ş Š Ș all follow it (ink width 483 Regular, 480 Bold).
  - The solver's S multiplier converges to 1.1245 (Regular) and 0.997 (Bold);
    no clamp binds.
  - Only `g_S` calls `W_(c, 'S')`, and `S_WT`'s default is exactly 1.0.
- **λ and χ:** advance and lsb are identical between rounds 464 and 472 in
  both italics.
- **`fences.py` partial mode:** a run with no flags behaves as the old code.
  Keys split on spaces, so there are no substring matches.
- **Also clean:** hmtx lsb equals glyf xMin everywhere; `approved.py --check`
