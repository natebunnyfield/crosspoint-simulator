## The first 442 was worse, and why (ruling 6)

`d9b18ef` shipped nothing, but it is in the history. Three misreadings produced it, each from an instrument answering a question nobody meant to ask.

1. **The arm was made heavy.**
   - Ruling 1's "line contrast" was read off the survey's whole-letter p10, which put F2's r hairline (24.1) below the word's a / f / t (32–47). Those letters are built differently.
   - The r's sibling is the n. F2's arm equalled the n's shoulder (23.3 against 24.1), because `R_SHO_K` was solved for exactly that on 2026-09-27.
   - Thickening the arm (`R_TK` 1.15) made the r the lowest-contrast letter in the word: stem ÷ climb 2.21 against the n's 2.62. Coelacanth's r goes the other way: its arm is finer than its own n's.
   - Ruling 5 caught it in the Bold Italic; the Italic had the same fault.
2. **The end-to-stem ratio was flattered.**
   - The "stem" band (0.10–0.45 xh) contains the join where the arm leaves the stem, so a heavier arm inflated the stem reading. The review found the Bold Italic's 0.91 → 0.93 came from the stem reading, not the end.
   - Measured below the join (0.10–0.30 xh), Albo's italic stem is 128.5 ‰ xh, not 149–157. Against it, every Albo r end (441 1.36, F2 1.42) is already heavier than Coelacanth's 1.25.
   - **The ratio cannot say what "optically correct" is here. Do not size this end by it.**
3. **"Reduced" was done by shortening the face with the junction held.**
   - `R_TF` 0.21 with `R_TJF` 0.60 turned the underside into a long flat and the end into a stub.
   - At F2's arm weight, P2a's end reads 1.40–1.43 of the (join-contaminated) stem whatever the arm width into it, so width alone cannot reduce it. Face length and junction set it.

**What changed for the revision:** the arm went back to the n's shoulder in both italics. The end kept P2a's compactness (hang about 0.70), at a weight between F2 and P2a:
- the face at 249 (between 246 and 252);
- the full face length 0.30;
- the junction moved toward the tip (0.64).

## Spacing

Round 441's white after the r is held for every r + letter, figure and common punctuation mark (`. , : ; ? ! ' " ’ ” - – — …`). Through `kern.py` it also covers **every accented composite of those letters and ŕ ř ŗ as the first letter** (below).

**The measure follows the geometry** (`instruments/r_hold_white.py`):
- **Letters and figures: the closest approach**, the measure rounds 438–441 set the owner's r + letter targets in.
- **Exceptions: the letters whose top reaches over the r's end** (`f T X Z 7`), and **every punctuation mark**. These hold the smallest HORIZONTAL gap over the rows both glyphs ink, and use the closest approach when no row is shared.
- **Why:** the nearest point of those glyphs lies above or below the end. That distance is vertical, and a kern barely moves it. Held by closest approach, the T was dragged 21–26 units sideways and a baseline period 26 units away (the second review caught the T; the period was caught in pass 1).
- **Why the row gap is not used on letters in general:** its minimum jumps to whichever row the new end reaches. `r d` read −18 and `r 1` −65 by rows, where the closest approach moved +4 and +3.

**How it was set:**
- `R_DRSB` −16 / −15 holds the stems (ru rn ri rm within 1.6).
- The `rsnip` block holds the rest: 45 Italic and 37 Bold Italic per-pair kerns, from round 441's kerns in one pass.
- Every held pair is within 2.5 units of 441's.
- `r T` / `r X`: 4 / -8 (Italic) and 3 / -7 (Bold Italic), held by row gap. By closest approach they read looser (Italic `rT` 188 → 207), because the end's crest now sits on the o's line (444) instead of 441's flag at 460. That is deliberate.

**Composites follow their base (`kern.py` `_extend_holds`, run in `apply()`).**
- Each hold is given to every accented composite of its follower (é è ê ë for e, á à for a, Ť for T …) and to ŕ ř ŗ on the left, by Unicode decomposition over the font's own cmap. That is what a kerning class would do.
- **A gap dating from round 438 closes with it:** the holds never reached accented letters, so in 441 `ré` sat 25 units looser than `re` (118.5 against 93.2) and `rá` 37 looser than `ra`.
- Now, Italic: `re` 94.0, `ré` 100.6 (the acute is nearer than the bowl), `ra` 111.5, `rá` 118.2, `ŕe` / `ře` 100.6. Bold Italic `ré` 104.8.
- **Accented pairs therefore move against 441, on purpose:** toward their bases, not held at 441's inconsistency.

**Deliberately NOT held:**
- `r t`: back at round 437's value; 441's was 5–7 looser as a side effect.
- The fence closes: `fences.py`'s symmetry rule governs them. Re-derived for the r alone on an `ALBO_FENCES=0` build (`instruments/r_fence_only.py`): Italic ±6 / ±4 / ±5, Bold Italic ±8 / ±6 / ±5.
- The rare math symbols `% = & # @ $ / \ < > ^ ~ ` * + |`: the row gap lands on their thin bars and is not a measure there.

clearance.py: nothing to add.

## Negative results (do not re-propose)

- **"More on it" by a longer face and a fuller arm** (`R_TF` 0.44, `R_TJW` 4.0) grows the end down and in. *"not like that"*.
- **A heavier arm on the r** (`R_TK` > 1) makes it the flattest letter in the word. The r's arm belongs at the n's shoulder, or finer (Coelacanth).
- **The survey's whole-letter p10 / p90 cut is not a line-contrast measure for one stroke.** Its thin is held by serif tips and facets. Use `r_climb_contrast.py`.
- **End ÷ stem with the stem band 0.10–0.45 xh is contaminated by the arm's join.** It is not a sizing rule for this end.
- **Shortening the face with the junction fixed makes a stub.** Scale the face and the junction together.
- **Holding a baseline or high mark by closest approach chases a vertical distance.** Use the row gap.

## Instruments (all run from `tools/wedge_serif`)

- `instruments/r_match_ref.py SLANT FONT [SLANT FONT ...]` (venv python)
  - The r against its own font's letters: end, hang, r/n, cut, col, and the white after r over the white after n.
  - Coelacanth takes slant 14.5.
- `instruments/r_climb_contrast.py FONT ...` (venv python)
  - The arm's climb hairline against the stem, for r and n. **The** line-contrast measure for the r.
- `instruments/r_end_stem.py FONT ...` (venv python)
  - The end's and the stem's largest inscribed circles. Mind the stem band (above).
- `instruments/r_live_measure.py` (asdf python, from the cut's env)
  - End / hang / end-to-stem / cut from the LIVE builder, in seconds. It reads +10 on the end and +0.03 on the hang against a built font.
- `instruments/r_fence_only.py BUILD_DIR` (venv python)
  - fences.py's rule for the r's six pairs, on an `ALBO_FENCES=0` build.
- `instruments/words_shaped.py OUT.png XHPX "LABEL::FONT::text" ...` (venv python)
  - HarfBuzz-shaped word rows (kerning applied). `word_rows.py` reads only a legacy `kern` table, which Albo does not have.
- The hold solver: `instruments/r_hold_white.py REF_DIR NEW_DIR` (`MODE=report` to only measure).
