## The passes

One instrument, `tools/wedge_serif/instruments/r_match_ref.py` (venv python), measures Albo and Coelacanth the same way:
- **end:** the largest inscribed circle, ‰ of the font's x-height;
- **hang:** the end's lowest ink ÷ x-height;
- **r/n:** the r's advance over the n's;
- **cut:** the survey's thick / thin;
- **col:** the r's colour over the median of n a e o u;
- **white after the r, over the white after an n:** closest approach, then mean row white, for a e o n i t.

**Pass 1 (the halfway reach alone).**
- Colour in words and the closest white after the r already matched Coelacanth.
- The end was 20 short (197) and hung too low (0.62).

**Pass 2 (the end).**
- At the ruled reach, with F2's 246° face, no combination of face length, arm width, junction and tip height passed about 209 on the built-font scale. That was 72 variants searched on the live builder with `instruments/r_live_measure.py` (asdf python; it reads +10 on the end and +0.03 on the hang against a built font).
- The reach was the strongest lever and it was fixed by the ruling. The face angle was the next: B20b's upright face had made a much thicker end.
- At 252°, a short face and a fuller arm reach 220 and hang at 0.67.
- In the unsheared overlay the end then fills Coelacanth's end almost exactly, with facets instead of the lobe.

**Pass 3 (Bold Italic).**

- Kept at 1.15 (cut 2.50, end 223): among its bold letters the r is open by nature.

## Line contrast (ruling 1)

- F2's r had the thinnest stroke in the word images: survey thin 24.1 against the word's letters' 26–47, cut 2.72.
- `R_TK` (new, multiplies the drawn-terminal arm's width keys on top of the solved `R_SHO_K`) at 1.15 brings the cut to 2.37 (Italic) and 2.50 (Bold Italic). The word's letters run 1.65–2.91 (Italic, n 2.40, e 2.44) and 1.68–3.20 (Bold Italic, e 2.43, a 2.48).

## Negative results (do not re-propose)

- **"More on it" by a longer face and a fuller arm** (`R_TF` 0.44, `R_TJW` 4.0, `R_TTX` 1.07) grows the end down and in. Owner: *"not like that"*.
- **A heavier Bold Italic arm does not darken the r** (above).
- **At F2's 246° face the end cannot reach Coelacanth's weight** at the ruled reach (pass 2).
- **Matching a reference's absolute per-mille of the x-height** makes the end optically heavy wherever Albo's stem differs from the reference's. Match the END-TO-STEM ratio (pass 5).
- **The survey's whole-letter cut is not a line-contrast measure for one stroke.** Measure the stroke (pass 6).
