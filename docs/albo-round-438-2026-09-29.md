**What ships** (both italics; the Regular and Bold are untouched):
- **The arm** (`aldine.py`): the drawn arch with no beak, stopped by one square cut.
  - `ALBO_ALD_R_DRAW` 1, `R_SNIP` 1.
  - The cut face `R_SWE` 4.4 (400) / 3.7 (700), ×1.7 option 1's (option 9).
  - The reach `R_SX` 0.95 → **1.03** of the pitch, the corner out. At 10 pt on the X3 the arm's corner now shows as an r's.
  - `ALBO_ALD_R_DRAW=0` restores round 437's flag.
- **The white after it** (`kern.py`, new `rsnip` block in `spacing_b2.json`, per cut, applied after fences and before clearance).
  - **Why:** the snipped arm sits inside the x-band the fit reads, so the fit widened the r, and the white before a ROUND letter grew (Italic ra 121 → 140 units closest approach). Before a stem it did not.
  - **The kerns:** r + a round letter goes back to round 437's closest approach, and r + a / e / o goes 10 units tighter than that:
    - Italic: a −38, e −24, o −29, c −10, d −13, q −12, s −4.
    - Bold Italic: a −13, e −8, o −12.
  - **Measured, closest approach:**

    | | ra | re | ro |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | Italic, round 437 | 121 | 103 | 108 |

    c / d / s are within 3 units of round 437.
  - **Rejected:** moving the r's right bearing (`R_DRSB`). −30 closed the vowels and jammed the stems (Italic ri 50 → 24), and it would have dragged the Greek σ, which borrows the r's right side (`build.py` `GREEK_SIDES`). σ is unchanged.
- **Stems after the r:** unchanged in the Italic. In the Bold Italic they are ~17 units tighter than round 437 (ri 51 → 34), from option 9's arm; the touch gate is clean.
- **Clearance:** Bold Italic `r "` +7 and `r '` +11.
