# Albo round 403: the lighter apostrophe (2026-09-26)

Owner ruling, 2026-09-26, after seeing the recommendation image (today against
HEAD 0.85 / STRAIGHT_W 0.75, roman and italic, curly and straight): **"yes to
everything but the straight italic apostrophe. make it match the style
better."**

The measurement and the options: `docs/albo-apostrophe-2026-09-26.md`.

## 1. What changed

| dial (`outlines/glyphs/marks.py`) | before | round 403 |
|---|---|---|
| `ALBO_APOS_HEAD`, the curly `’ ‘ ʼ ʻ` dot | 1.0 | **0.85**, every cut |
| `ALBO_APOS_STRAIGHT_W`, the straight `'`'s thickness | 1.0 | **0.75** in Regular and Bold; **1.0** in Italic and BoldItalic (unchanged, awaiting a redraw) |
| `ALBO_APOS_DOUBLES` | off | off. `“ ” "` are unchanged |

`spacing_b2.json` and `kern.py` were not touched. The marks' bearings are taken
off their ink extent, so the horizontal white beside each apostrophe is held.
The mark's advance shrinks:

- `’`: 305 → 298 (Regular), 189 → 180 (Italic);
- `'`: 247 → 225 (Regular).

The judged pairs' 2-D white moves by at most 6 units (apostrophe doc §6).

What it measures (Regular / Italic; reference median in brackets):

| | before | round 403 |
|---|---|---|
| `’` head, as a fraction of the stem | 1.86 / 1.85 | 1.61 / 1.56 (1.54 / 1.39) |
| `’` ink, as a fraction of an n's | 0.204 / 0.202 | 0.167 / 0.168 (0.185 / 0.182) |
| `’` height, as a fraction of the x-height | 0.554 / 0.497 | 0.533 / 0.482 (0.560 / 0.521) |
| `'` head / stem, Regular | 1.38 | 1.03 (1.14) |
| `'` ink / n, Regular | 0.252 | 0.191 (0.165) |
