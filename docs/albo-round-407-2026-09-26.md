For the italic he asked for variations on arm `p`. Those are round 408.

## What changed

The change is one line in `tools/wedge_serif/outlines/glyphs/figures.py`:

```
FIG_SHIP_ROM.update({'2': 'q', '7': 'p'})
```

- **The 2 is `TWO_TRACE_ROM['q']`, traced from Hoefler Text.** It uses
  Palatino's oblique nib axis (`stress` −30), so the crown reads 0.80 of the
  n stem against today's 0.62. The slash is lighter (0.48 against 0.61) and so
  is the base (0.84 against 0.87). The base runs 37 units past the arc. The left
  terminal hangs less (0.32 of the height against 0.41).
- **The 7 is `SEVEN_TRACE_ROM['p']`, traced from Palatino.** The leg swells
  from 0.54 of the n under the bar to 0.84 at the foot, where today's leg is
  even at 0.67. The foot sits at 0.16 of the width and the leg at 27.8° from
  vertical, against today's 0.30 and 23°. The beak is 114 units against 43. The
  top and the descender are today's.
- **Regular and Bold share `FIG_SHIP_ROM` and both change. The italic does not
  change.**
