# Albo round 467: smaller tittles, D2 (2026-10-02)

Owner, in order:

1. Todo: *"reduce tittles slightly without losing them at small scale"*.
2. Options D1 / D2 / D3 (`docs/albo-tittles-2026-10-02.md`, proof page linked
   there).
3. **Ruling:** *"D2 wins"*.

**Ships:** `ALBO_TITTLE_K` 0.90 with `ALBO_TITTLE_DY` 5 in the Regular, and
`ALBO_TITTLE_K_700` 0.90 with `_DY_700` 0 in the Bold
(`outlines/glyphs/stems.py`).
- They scale the roman i's and j's dot 10% about its floor, lifting the
  Regular's 5 units.
- They do the same to the dots in ﬁ, ﬃ, ĳ and U+E002.
- They apply to the roman cuts only.
- The dot accents keep their size.
- K 1.0 with DY 0 builds round 466's dots.

**What it costs at small sizes**, the trade D2 was chosen with
(`instruments/parts_check.py`):
- On the X3, the Regular i at 10 and 12 pt and the Regular j at 12 pt go from
  4 dark pixels to 3.
- Every other raster stays at or above 4, or at today's count where today was
  already under.
- The Bold loses nothing.

## The italic ligatures, caught before shipping

The first default build also moved ﬁ, ﬃ and U+E002 **in the italic cuts**.
The italics encode those glyphs, which `ligatures.py` draws with the same dot
(the italic does no ligature substitution, round 456; the glyphs are reached
only by their own codepoints). The D2 arm the owner judged took its italics
from round 466, so shipping that build would have shipped something he never
saw.

The dials are now gated on `not pen.ITALIC`. Rebuilt, the Italic and the Bold
Italic are identical to round 466, and POOR GATES' delta (the italic
ligatures' glitch sweep) is gone.
