What this round fixes: the evenness of his common word images, the objective
of `docs/albo-word-image-drivers-2026-10-09.md`. Every finding here is MINE,
from measurement; the owner said none of it (2026-10-10: *"I DID NOT SAY

words", meaning measured in the words from his books; the phrase is retired).

Page: https://claude.ai/artifact/AXpkEK7uL1RDTTzcBGU1Zv

## The instrument

`tools/wedge_serif/instruments/word_drivers.py MEASURE.json [--compare]`:
the decomposition from a `word_measure.py` JSON -- the top-100 gap
(token-weighted unevenness over the references' median), the count and token
share of words less even than every reference, and each letter's slot
deviation and share of the total. It reproduces the 2026-10-09 tables to the
digit on `measure-A.json` (o 20.4%, a 18.8%, f 12.0%).

- **Narrowing the straight letters (N) is wrong**: they darken and the i
  (which has no counter to shrink) reads -10%; one cause, but the fix is not
  the counter.
- **Widening the diagonals (W) fixes the w and nothing else**, and the gap
  moves the wrong way.
- **The white is the lever** (T/T2): the references' own Regular -> Bold
  move. It is a bearing change outside the bench's tables, so it is an option
  for the owner and not a fitted row. The t does not want it (+4.0%), so T2.
- The h (-5.6%) and the s (+5.8%) survive every arm: the h is a WIDE straight
  letter the references do not tighten, and the s is a drawn spine, not a
  bowl.

## Process notes (what cost time)

- `export -f` exports a bash function, not an array: the first batch built
  all 14 arms with no `FJORD_STEM` env (every "Bold" was a Regular); caught
  by `cmp_outlines` on the identity arm (354/511 glyphs differed), fixed by
  re-sourcing `build_env.sh` inside the xargs subshell.

  build moved only the i. `ALBO_BOLD_SB_ADJ` now also takes "/".
- With both fixed, the identity arm (no env) rebuilds build 318's Regular and
  Bold IDENTICAL (cmp_outlines, 0 of 530).
