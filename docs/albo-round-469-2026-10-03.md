# Albo round 469: the Regular S widened to match the Bold (2026-10-03)

The owner was asked "2 of 4" of the queued questions. The question said the
Regular S is narrow in the same way the Bold S was: 0.54 of its H's width,
where six regular references run 0.59–0.66 (median 0.63). Since round 465 the
two weights' S's no longer matched. **Ruling:** *"Match the Bold, ship."* (He
picked that over an options page.)

**Ships:** `ALBO_ROM_S_W` 1.20 (`outlines/build.py`, `CAP_S_WIDTH_REG`). It is
the Bold's round-465 factor on the same width-solver target, for stems 84 and
under (the Regular). Setting `ALBO_ROM_S_W=1.0` builds round 468's letter.

## What changed

| Regular S | round 468 | round 469 | Bold S (round 465) |
|---|---|---|---|
| S / H width | 0.540 | **0.643** | 0.642 |
| S / E width | 0.738 | 0.879 | 0.881 |
| S / O height | 0.994 | 1.001 | 1.006 |

Measured by `instruments/bold_s_proof.py --measure`.
- The regular references run S/H 0.59–0.66 and S/E 0.78–0.87; Georgia,
  Charter and Times are 0.66, 0.65 and 0.64.
- A ladder of 1.18 / 1.20 / 1.22 measured 0.632 / 0.643 / 0.655, and 1.20 is
  the one on the Bold.
- As in the Bold, the S keeps its fitted bearings and kerns, and Ś, Š, Ş, Ŝ,
  Ș, $ and § follow it.
