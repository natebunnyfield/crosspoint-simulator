- The Bold's leg is twice its arm's weight, so its upper edge meets the arm
  nearer the stem than the Regular's does for the same spring point. Moving
  the spring point out (u 0.31) puts the crotch at the Regular's distance
  from the stem exactly; lowering the arm's root (0.42) brings its height
  back to within 13 units of the Regular's, with the arm leaving the stem
  within 7.
- Matching all three to the unit would need a lighter leg at the junction,
  which reopens the K's ruled kick (2026-09-13: *"leave K R Q M W as is"*).
  Not done.
