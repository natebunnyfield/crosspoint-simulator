# Albo round 391: thick and thin balanced (2026-09-25)

Owner, looking at 2x word images of "relationship", "everything",
"engineering" in roman and italic: *"take a pass at balancing out the thick
and thin strokes for better word images"*.

Built on round 390 (`0c02ee9`). Four letters' worth of change in two places,
chosen as the clearest outliers; everything else measured and left alone
(section 4).

## 1. The instrument

`cmp_weight_survey.py --json` on the four cuts (chamfer ridge, unsheared,
xh 429, no percentile threshold on the ridge), regrouped by CONSTRUCTION
FAMILY with the misfit audit's map (round `acegos CGOQS`, diag `vwxyz
AVWXYZ`, figure `0-9`, stem the rest) by the new
`tools/wedge_serif/instruments/r391_thick_thin.py`:

- **thin** = p10 of ridge thickness, **thick** = p90, **cut** = thick/thin,
  **colour** = ink in the reading band / (advance × reference height);
- each letter against its family median (dThin, dCut, dCol);
- **px27** = the thin in pixels at a 27 px em (the 1x app reading size).

A ridge map (ridge points under 22 / 28 units drawn red) located WHERE each
letter's thin is, so a p10 was not taken on trust:

- roman `s`: the upper shoulder and the lower bowl, where the spine runs along
  the pen's thin; `t`: the tail's upturn; `y`: the tail's lower run; `e`: its
  exit; `n h m`: the arch's join; `k`: the arm; `x`: the thin diagonal.
- italic `h m n r u`: the arch's climb, i.e. `HM_ARCH_T`; `o a q p d b`: the
  bowls' thins.

## 3. What changed

### Italic: the arch hairline, `ALBO_ALD_HM_ARCH_T` 22 → 26

Round 132 drew the climb's hairline at 22 units, Flanker's lowercase hairline
(22–24, `albo-aldine-targets.md`) at the Medium's stem of 84. `ALD_WF` then
scaled every thickness by S/84, so at the shipping 400 (S 66.9) the hairline is
17.5 units, 0.47 px at the 1x reading size, and the arch letters are the
thinnest in the italic. 26 puts the 400's hairline at 20.7 and its
stem/hairline ratio at 2.69 (Flanker 70/23 = 3.0, Pagella 61/20.5 = 3.0), so
the italic stays the more contrasted style of the two.

Ladder 22 / 25 / 28, italic 400, survey p10:

| m thin / cut | 22.6 / 2.70 | 25.3 / 2.41 | 27.1 / 2.25 |
| n | 23.6 / 2.59 | 26.3 / 2.31 | 27.1 / 2.25 |

28 would take the arch letters to the stem family's cut and past Flanker's
absolute hairline on a stem 20% lighter than Flanker's; 26 is the value
between, picked on the 27 px ×4 crops. The `u` moves with the family (its rise
is drawn at 1.25–1.45 × the same hairline).

### Roman: a hairline floor on three pen-drawn thins, 0.33 × S

New dials, each `pen_widths(..., floor=S × dial)`, **0 reproduces round 390
byte for byte** (measured: 0 of 530 glyphs differ):

- `ALBO_ROM_S_FLOOR` (`stems.py`, the s's spine);
- `ALBO_ROM_T_TAIL_FLOOR` (`stems.py`, the t's tail);
- `ALBO_ROM_Y_TAIL_FLOOR` (`diagonals.py`, the y's tail, roman only).

The italic draws its own `s t y` in `aldine.py` and is untouched by these.
The floor only lifts the troughs: every letter's stroke median and p90 are
unchanged, and colour moves by at most +0.004.

Ladder 0.30 / 0.33 / 0.36 (roman 400, thin / cut):

0.33 ships: each letter's thin to about 24 units (0.65 px at 27 px), still

Outlines moved: Regular and Bold 6 each — `s t y tbar uniE004 uniFB06`.

## 4. Checked and left alone

- **Roman `n h m` join** (n −28%): the owner lightened these joins on purpose
  (round 232, `N_JOIN_TAPER`, *"I wanted to slightly reduce the weight of the
  joins"*).
- **Roman `x`** (−30%) and **`k`** arm (−31%): the x's thin diagonal carries
  the owner's round-90 rulings (`X_BL_*`); the k is inside noise of the x.
  Neither is in the three words.
- **Roman `e`** (27.1, −20%): its bar and exit are thin, but under the 25%
  line. Not moved.
- **Italic `a e o`**: contrast is per letter by the owner's rulings
  (`ALBO_ALD_CON_A/E/O`, 2026-09-15/16, `albo-aldine-metrics.md`). Not
  touched.
- **Italic `s` and `y`** (−28%, −29%): their own constructions in `aldine.py`
  with ruled dials. Not touched this round.
- **Italic `q p d b`** bowls (−26 to −32%): a bowl thin, not the arch; a
  separate construction. Candidates for a later pass.
- **The approved g's**: untouched (`approved.py --check` unchanged).
- **Colour**: the italic arch letters were already darker than their family
  (h +17%, m +11%) and go +2–3% darker. That is the cost of the hairline, and
  it is small.
- **Figures** (roman `3 8`, italic `6` flagged THIN): not letters in the
  owner's words, and the figures have their own option system. Not touched.
