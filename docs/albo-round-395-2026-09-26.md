## 2. Two things that had to be held still

**The italic 8 does not move.** Its option `x` is solved onto the 6's line
(`to_six='shipped'`, round 374), so the italic 6's new tail end would have
raised the italic 8 too. Round 394's F arms showed it moving. The solve now
measures the 6 at `tail_end=0.0`, which is the round-393 run-out. The italic
and BoldItalic `eight` are outline- and advance-identical to round 393
(`cmp_outlines`).

**The 8 pick is roman only.** The owner picked `ALBO_8_NIB` "1.03,0.30,0".
That env var reaches both styles, so it is shipped instead as the roman
option `z`: the same dict as `x` with the nib's thin at 0.30. The italic keeps
`x`.

**The italic λ does not move.** The k was not picked, and `ALBO_ROM_K_ARM` is
roman-only in any case.

**The defaults reproduce the slider renders.** Each picked glyph was hashed
against its slider step's build, and all 10 are identical: the roman 8, 3, x,
Y and e, and the italic a, q, s, y and 6.
