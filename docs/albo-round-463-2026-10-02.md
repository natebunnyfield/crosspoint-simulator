Owner ruling (`docs/local-ai-spacing-options-2026-09-26.md` §14c): *"Refresh and
refit now."* That was the choice among refreshing and refitting at the next
bench, refreshing and refitting now, or leaving it.

**Status: TWO REFIT ARMS (B, C) beside today (A), awaiting a pick.** Nothing
shipped. The default build is still build 283's spacing, byte for byte: the
shipped `spacing_b2.json` and the three hand corrections at their shipped

## 1. What was stale

B2 re-bases every italic reading onto the italic's current outline by
comparing two builds that differ only in the outline
(`local_ai/b2_fit.py`, round 409). Those builds, in
`bench/italic-delta-r409/`, carry round 409's outline. Every italic letter
redrawn since was therefore re-based against its OLD shape:

- the a (round 419);
- the c's drawn top (rounds 432–435);
- the r (rounds 438–442);
- the x (round 461);
- small changes to e and j.

Three of those redraws needed a hand bearing to hold their whites:
`ALBO_ALD_A_RSB` −18, `ALBO_ALD_R_DRSB` −16 / −15 and round 461's
`X_DLSB` / `X_DRSB`.

## 4. The refit (§14b, end to end)

Fit into a scratch table, then merge letters, marks, kerns, scope and
in_sample. Then:

1. **Holds** re-measured against build 283 on a build with them cleared (the
   hold step writes each pair's full compensation). The roman holds came out
   identical.
2. **Fences** regenerated on an `ALBO_FENCES=0` build. The roman's are
   unchanged; about 100 italic fence kerns move a few units each.
3. **Clearance loop** (base, composites, 2-D marks), alternated with rebuilds
   until all three add 0. Two passes each arm. The first pass re-tunes about
   170 mark clearances per italic cut to the new bearings.
4. **Gates, each arm against build 283:**
   - POOR GATES: no delta (touch 0 / 0 all cuts, hairs none, contours 1056,
     approved unchanged);
   - kern classes ok (Italic 124 / 159, Bold Italic 124 / 160);
   - GATES UNCHANGED.

The roman does not move in either arm (it never takes the re-basing).

## 5. The two arms

Both retire the three hand corrections; the model now places those letters
from his readings. Both keep the r-snip kerns, which the owner asked for
(round 438, "fill the space between r and the next letter").

- **B, the full refit.** Every redraw re-based.
- **C, the refit with the c held** (`ALBO_B2_REBASE_SKIP=c`). The c's own
  share of the outline change is not re-based; every other letter's is.

**Movement against build 283** (Italic; the model's own band white; share of
letter-pair occurrences in his 41 books):

| moved by | B | C |
|---|---|---|
| >= 5 units | 28.0% | 23.6% |
| >= 10 | 18.9% | 11.8% |
| >= 20 | 4.3% | 0.8% |

**Largest movers.**
- **B:** co +43, ch +41, ca +52, ce +45, ct +42, ci +43, ck +38, cu +42,
  cr +44, cl +40; ra +20, re +13, ri +14, ro +13; an +10, ha +14, al +10,
  as +11; and the rare capital + y pairs about −26 (My, Sy, By, Cy).
- **C:** ra +20, re +13, ri +11, ro +11, rs +13, rt +11; an +10, ha +14,
  al +12, ag +18; ca only +9; the same rare capital + y pairs about −24.

**Why B's c is suspect.** The band white is measured between the letters'
extreme ink, and the c's drawn top ends in a terminal tip that reaches
right. Restoring the old extreme-to-extreme gap therefore pushes the c's
whole BODY 44 units further from the next letter. At 10 pt on the X3 every c
in B stands detached ("Ea ch", "ch apter", "c rowd"). This is the
"a letter's own open white belongs to the letter" failure of
`docs/albo-spacing-method.md`. It is also why round 461's x correction, which
used the same measure, deserves a second look in words.

**The rare y pairs** (gy −26, capital + y −24 to −26) come from the fit's
y identity term (+4 → −22). Common y pairs are in scope and keep their
kerns; pairs under the census floor take bearings only.

**The r and a.** Most of their +8 to +20 is the retired −16 and −18
returning: the refit, on today's outlines, does not ask for them back.

## 6. Reproduce an arm

    cd tools/wedge_serif; source build_env.sh
    env "${ALBO_ITA_ENV[@]}" ALBO_SPACING_TABLES=spacing_b2_r463C.json ALBO_ALD_X_DLSB=0 ALBO_ALD_X_DRSB=0 \
        ALBO_ALD_R_DRSB=0 ALBO_ALD_R_DRSB_700=0 ALBO_ALD_A_RSB=0 PYTHON_GIL=0 python3 -m outlines.build OUT --style Italic
    (BoldItalic the same with ALBO_BIT_ENV; Regular and Bold need no flags)
    $VENV/bin/python instruments/spacing_arms.py PROOF A=<build 283> B=<arm B> C=<arm C>

which runs §14b end to end (`ALBO_B2_REBASE_SKIP=c` for arm C).
