# Albo round 446: the italic r's end, a pen edge, not a beak (2026-09-30)

Owner, on round 445 (TestFlight build 274): *"improve further to be less of a beak and more pen edge"*.

## What was a beak

445 stood the face up to 262°, so the bottom grew right. An upright cut meeting a rising underside makes a point that hangs down. That point is the beak.

## What changed

**The face is the nib's own edge.**
- Albo's italic pen sits at 50° (`nib()`, phi 50), so a stroke that stops is cut by a line at 50° / 230°.
- `R_TFA` 262 → **232** in both italics.
- The end now reads as a broad-nib stroke cut by the pen: the point is gone, and the filed corner takes the sharpness off the cut's lower end.

**Supporting changes:**
- Face length `R_TF` 0.30 / **0.34** (Bold Italic).
- Stroke into the cut, `R_TJW` 2.8 / 3.1 → **3.4 / 3.9**.
- Filed corner `R_TCH` 1.5 → **2.0 / 1.2**. The Bold Italic's smaller corner keeps its short underside from being eaten into a jog.
- Unchanged: the tip, the reach, the crest, 443's notch and join, and the arm's hairline (stem ÷ climb 2.62 / 2.60).

**Negative results (do not re-propose):**
- **A curved underside flowing out of the arm (`R_TUB`)** cannot work with a nib-angle cut. The cut's lower end falls below-left of the notch, so the curve must loop back to meet the arm. That leaves a thin white slit (Italic) or a stepped tooth (Bold Italic), seen at 3× zoom.
- **The end drawn as the pen stroke itself** (the snipped arm, `R_TERM=0`, cut at 230) grows a small hook under the cut. That hook is a beak again.
- **P4** (a longer nib cut with no filed corner) grows a thin sliver at its lower tip.

## Spacing

The cut now slants away from the next letter, which opened the white after the r by 15–34 units (Italic r+a +34). Held at round 441's by `instruments/r_hold_white.py` in four passes: the nearest points now lie on a diagonal, so each pass closes the gap by less than its full kern. Every held pair is within 2.5 units. The r's fence pairs re-derive unchanged, and clearance adds nothing.
