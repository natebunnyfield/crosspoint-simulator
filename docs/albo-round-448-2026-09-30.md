**Albo was the only face where the two disagree.** Rounds 441–447 tuned the r's end as a letter on its own, against the r's of other faces. That was the wrong comparison: the right one is the r against its own c. Checked against source; the reference readings are by eye at 170–220 px x-height, and all four are unambiguous.

This is also what "pen edge and metal punch like rest" (2026-09-29) asks for, read literally. The rest of Albo's italic finials are drawn wedges with one straight cut. The c's top is the one the r corresponds to, stroke for stroke.

## Found by adversarial review, not fixed (recorded so it is not re-found)

- **`r?` is looser in the Bold Italic.** Its `rsnip` delta went −19 → +28 (Italic −4 → +9); the delta sits on top of the base kern, and the Bold Italic's total `r?` kern is now +2. The hold measures `?` by the tightest horizontal gap over the rows both glyphs share. With the new end, that gap sits between the end's lower corner and the `?`'s neck (row 310 Bold Italic, 323 Italic); at 446 it sat at the crest (rows 413–414). That gap is held exactly, but:
  - the closest 2-D approach rose 99.9 → 140.7 in the Bold Italic (441: 101.3) and 108.9 → 123.0 in the Italic (441: 96.2);
  - the mean gap over shared rows grew by 43 units in the Bold Italic, about one X3 pixel.

  The pair does not touch (`cmp_touch`: 0) and sits inside the lowercase range before `?` (Bold Italic `a?` 105 … `s?` 147); it reads like `e?` and `t?`. Left as held. A proper fix is a punctuation measure that tracks the whole shared band, not its tightest row.
- **ŕ ř ŗ never receive the r's base kerns (pre-existing since round 441, not caused by 448).** `kern.py`'s `_extend_holds` passes only the `rsnip` deltas to the composites, so the base kern is missing from each. For example, `r.` −90 against `ř.` −8 in the Italic, and `ř?` +28 against `r?` +2 in the Bold Italic. At 441 it was already `r.` −82 against `ř.` 0. It needs the composites to inherit the base r's full pair kerns (class kerning or an explicit copy), which is a change to `kern.py` and out of this round's scope.
