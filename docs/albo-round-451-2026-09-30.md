## The ogonek: a hook, not a wiggle

**What was wrong.** Laid beside Pagella, Georgia, Times and Coelacanth at 150 px x-height (`instruments/acc_sheet.py`), every reference grows a sturdy hook out of the letter's foot that sweeps DOWN AND LEFT, turns at the bottom and curls back RIGHT to a fine tip: heavy at the root, tapering out. Albo's (round 99, resized in 450) was a thin stroke running down and to the RIGHT from the foot, a wiggle that read as detached.

**The drawing** (`accents.py` `g_ogonek`, under `ALBO_OGO_HOOKED`, default on with `ALBO_ACC_FIT`):
- **Centerline:** one cubic from the attach point (0, 0), out left to the bowl and back right to the tip.
  - `OGO_BOWL` 0.85 of the depth: how far left the hook sweeps.
  - `OGO_HD` 0.52 of the x-height deep.
  - `OGO_TIP` 0.42 of the depth: how far right of the attach the tip ends.
  - `OGO_HOOK` 0.60: how far the tip curls back up.
- **Width:** 0.86 → 1.0 → 0.86 → 0.24 of `OGO_W` × stem along the path, so heavy at the root and fine at the tip. `OGO_W` is 0.90 at the 400 and `OGO_W7` 0.66 at the 700, eased by stem.
- **No pen cuts.** The square ends here are the stroke's own.

**Passes:**
- **A1 (depth 0.40, bowl 0.42):** right direction, too small and shallow.
- **A2 (0.48, 0.55, hook 0.45):** a hook, but narrow in the bowl and short in the curl.
- **o1, o2, o3:** three rounder, hookier arms. **o2 ships**: the widest bowl and the most curl, for *"more hooky"*.

**Placement** (`build.py`): the ogonek hangs by its ATTACH point, the middle of its own top (`inktop`).
- **On i, I and the dotless i:** at the stem's middle.
- **Elsewhere:** `OGO_IN` 0.15 of the x-height inside the letter's rightmost foot ink.
- **Overlap:** it reaches `OGO_OVERLAP` 0.03 of the x-height up into the foot, so no hairline of white opens between letter and mark at reading sizes.
- **No slope correction in the italic.** The attach is measured at the foot itself, so the italic needs no correction. Round 450's below-mark correction still applies to the cedilla and the comma below.

## The tilde: an even wave, lighter in the bolds

**What was wrong.** Round 450's tilde (the sweep's option b) was still drawn ON THE PEN:
- the nib's thick on every falling stretch and its thin on every rising one;
- pen-cut ends that curled into hooks;
- at the bold weights, a lumpy bird instead of a wave (see the page's tilde row, "B 450").

Every reference draws a smooth wave: thin at both ends, full through the middle, point-symmetric.

**The drawing** (`g_tilde`, `ALBO_TILDE_EVEN`):
- **Centerline:** one point-symmetric cubic, (0, 0.25H) → (0.30W, 1.35H) → (0.70W, −0.35H) → (W, 0.75H), in a box `TILDE_EW` 0.66 × `TILDE_EH` 0.40 of the x-height.
- **Width:** a sine across it, the ends at `TILDE_ENDS` 0.30 of the middle. No pen modulation and no cut.
- **Weight:** mean width `TILDE_MW` 0.60 of the stem at the 400, easing to `TILDE_MW7` at the 700.

**The bold weight, laddered:**
- **The ladder:** `TILDE_MW7` 0.30, 0.36, 0.42 and 0.48, in B and Z, beside round 450's.
- **What it showed:** 0.30–0.36 read as hairlines beside the bold letters, 0.48 heavy through the middle, and 0.42 sat with the bold's own thin strokes.
- **0.40 ships**, leaning toward the lighter side the owner asked for.
- **For the record, the ratio rule was rejected.** Holding the Regular's tilde-to-hairline ratio (1.28; the o's thin top is 36 units) would make the bold's tilde 78 units mean, heavier than the 450 tilde the owner called too thick. The bolds take the owner's eye over the ratio. The Bold's o's thin top is 61 units, and its tilde is now about 46 units mean.

## The chevrons: one mitered polygon each

**What was wrong.** The circumflex, the caron, the 400s' guillemets « » ‹ › and the signs < > ≤ ≥ ^ were each two `stroke`s unioned at the point, each ending SQUARE there. The two square ends overlapped at an angle and left a stepped notch on the outer corner with a spur beside it. It was worst in the bolds, where the circumflex read as a broken hat. The 700s' guillemets were already one polygon (`symbols._chevron`, round 385) and were clean.

**The fix:** `primitives.miter_chevron(a, p, b, w1, w2, cut_a, cut_b)`. Each arm's edges are offset lines at its own width, so the pen's thick and thin arms miter cleanly. The outer edges meet at the miter point and the inner edges at the crotch, and the free ends are cut as `stroke` cuts them. The circumflex and caron take the pen's width for each arm's direction (`accents._chev`); the guillemets and the math signs take their existing monoline width. The 700 guillemets are unchanged.

## Fixed after round 450's adversarial review (same day)

The review ran on round 450's build. Seven findings; each is fixed here, measured on the final build.

1. **The italic comma below had fused into its S** (Italic Ș 22 → 1 unit of white, Bold Italic Ș overlapping; round 375 rules it stands free).
   - **Cause:** round 450's slope term for marks below also carried U+0326 left under the S's lower bowl.
   - **Fix:** the comma below is excluded from the term.
   - **Result:** Italic Ș 22, Bold Italic 11, every ș ț Ț free; the italic Ș ș Ț ț are back to round 448's outlines.
2. **Some ogoneks hung off their foot** (Italic ą 10, į 11, Ą 2, Į 5, Ų 4; Regular Ų 9; Bold Ų 4; Bold Italic Ą 6, ą 3).
   - **Cause:** the attach point was a fixed distance inside the rightmost foot ink, which fell past the foot.
   - **Fix:** the ogonek hangs from the base's own foot INK: the runs of ink in a thin band at the baseline (`footruns`). It takes the stem's run on i/I and the rightmost run elsewhere; a narrow run takes it at the middle, a wide one `OGO_IN` from its end.
   - **Result:** every ogonek overlaps its letter in all four cuts (gap 0).
3. **The italic įj +70 kern was an instrument artifact.** The j's tail passes UNDER the ogonek, so the row-wise white read −0.038 em while the true closest approach was +0.026. Clearance pushed the j 70 units through real contact.
   - **Fix:** the composite sweep takes the 2-D approach whenever the row-wise white is under 0.05 em, negative included, and reads overlapping ink as 0.
4. **Vertical metrics were checked against the wrong bound.** The reader lays Albo out on the ascent `sd-fonts.yaml` declares (1023), not on usWinAscent.
   - **Fix:** an above-mark comes down until its top is at most 1015 (`ACC_TOP_MAX`), never nearer the letter than 0.03 of the x-height.
   - **Result:** ĥ is 999 / 1013 / 1015 / 1020 (R / I / B / Z) and ĺ at most 1015. All are under 1023; the Z ĥ stops at 1020 on the minimum gap.
5. **The 450 doc said HarfBuzz ignores lsb. It does not:** uharfbuzz draws an outline with the same xMin = lsb shift FreeType makes. The SHAPED ADVANCES AND KERNS are what lsb does not touch, which is why every shaped measurement looked right. Corrected in the 450 doc.
6. **The bold dieresis and dot-above dots fell under parts_audit's 4-dark-pixel floor at 8 pt** on the X3: 18 cells at the square root.
   - **The ladder** (`DOT_WEXP`, the dots' growth with the stem; no dieresis merged at any reader size, 1x or 2x, at any rung):

     | `DOT_WEXP` | Failing cells |
     |---|---|
     | 0.50 | 18 |
     | 0.40 | 15 |
     | 0.30 | 5 |
     | 0.25 | 1 |
     | 0.20 | 1 |

   - **0.25 ships:** the bold dot is 0.34 of the x-height, the references' largest.
7. **The sweep never tried some neighbors:** `*`, đ, ø, ł, ß, æ, œ, þ, ð, ¿, ¡ and their capitals are now in its base set. The italic î* ï* ĩ* ī* ĵ* and the Bold Italic đš were touching unseen.
