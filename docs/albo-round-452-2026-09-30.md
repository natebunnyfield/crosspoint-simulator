## 1. The italic marks, centered on the stems

**The rule** (`outlines/build.py`, `stem_axis` and the `STEM_CENTER` branch; the ITALICS only, because the roman's box rule already measured with its references in round 450):
- **The axis comes from the letter's ink runs** in a thin band at 0.75 of its height (`STEM_AT`): the midpoint of the outer runs' centers. That is the two stems of n, u and h, the two sides of o, and the bowl and stem of a.
- **One run:** a single run is the stem (at 0.75 of the height only i, j and l read as one run; the h reads three), unless it is narrower than 0.45 of the letter's box, in which case it is a lone side and the BOX is the axis.
- **No stems, the box:** the z, and the c s C S G, whose second run at that height is a TERMINAL grazing the band rather than a stem, take the box outright (`BOX_ONLY_Z`). Before the review's F4 these took the box in the Italic only by the lone-run rule; in the Bold Italic the terminal slivers (12–28 units) made them a two-run letter and set ś at +0.11 against the references' +0.04 to +0.07. A width threshold cannot do this job: the italic X's real hairline is 15 units.
- **Carried up the slope:** the axis is carried along the slant (`pen.SHEAR`) from the height it was measured at to the mark's own centroid. A box axis is carried from the box's MID-height, which is where a sheared box's center is.
- **Placed by the ink centroid:** the mark goes there by its own ink centroid, plus `MARK_DX_IT` for the oblique marks: acute +0.06, grave −0.08, double acute +0.05 of the x-height. Every reference sets the acute right and the grave left.

## 2. The ogonek, traced

**What was wrong:** round 451's ogonek was a cubic of five dials with a width table laid along it. It read as constructed: a heavy root, and a hook whose weight sat in the wrong places.

**The trace** (`instruments/ogonek_trace.py`, which regenerates `outlines/glyphs/ogonek_traced.py`): this is not a copy of a bezier. What comes out is the STROKE, as `trace_g.py` did for the g on 2026-09-17.
- **The ogonek's ink:** each reference's a-ogonek and its a are rasterized at 600 px x-height, unhinted. An italic is unsheared by its MEASURED slant (Georgia Italic 13.0°, Times Italic 16.1°, Pagella Bold Italic 12.5°). The ogonek is the a-ogonek's ink minus the a's.
- **Centerline:** Zhang-Suen thinning, then the longest geodesic path. The skeleton forks where the ogonek meets the a, so that fork is trimmed and the path re-extended along its own tangent to the baseline. Every trace starts where the mark leaves the letter.
- **Width:** the inscribed circle's diameter (2 × the distance transform) through the body. Past the skeleton's end, the chord across the tip. In the root zone, where the circle is cut by the junction, the first clear width is held back to the root.
- **Fitted, not interpolated:** a least-squares cubic spline through x(s), y(s) and w(s), with 9 and 7 knots. Maximum distance from the raw skeleton: 0.004 x-height (under 2 units). Drawing the path THROUGH every raw sample put the skeleton's pixel noise onto the inner edge as wobble.
- **Units:** x and y in x-heights from the root; width in the reference's own stem, so the mark keeps its letter's colour at Albo's lighter stem.
- **Tables:**
  - **'pagella'** (the default) is TeX Gyre Pagella alone, under the GUST Font License (a derivative under another name is permitted): Regular and Bold for the roman, Italic and Bold Italic for the italic.
  - **'mean'** is Pagella, Georgia and Times averaged at equal arc length.
  - Flanker (a ball terminal hung from the bowl, a different construction) and Coelacanth (0.63 x-height deep) are traced and printed but left out.

**Drawing it in Albo** (`outlines/glyphs/accents.py`, `_ogo_traced` and `_brush`):
- **Weight:** the 400 and 700 traces blend by the stem, as every weight-dependent dial does.
- **The stroke:** the path is a Catmull-Rom curve through the 49 fitted samples, resampled every 4 units, and the width is a spline through the keys.
- **A brush sweep:** the stroke is the union of the hulls of consecutive width-circles. That is its exact envelope, and it resolves the hook's tight inner turn into the clean inner corner the references have. The tip is cut square across the path.
- **The tip's floor:** the width never falls below `OGO_TTIP` 0.20 of the stem. The Pagella traces end at 0.10–0.14 of their stem in the roman and 0.02–0.04 in the italic, a point; a point drawn at Albo's stem vanishes at reading sizes, so the italic tip is blunter than its source.
- **The root:** cut flat on the baseline.

**The three dead ends** (recorded so they are not tried again):
1. **`primitives.widths()` scalloped the edges.** It smoothsteps between keys, so its slope is zero at every key, and 25 keys terraced the width into a ripple wherever it changed. The traced width is a spline instead (`_cr_fn`).
2. **The tight inner turn folded.** Where the half-width passes the centerline's radius, one offset polygon's inner edge folds back on itself.
   - `stroke()`'s unfold left a kink there.
   - Capping the width at the radius pulled a chin out of the OUTER edge.
   - `stroke(pieces=True)` is broken for any fold: it pairs the two sides by index AFTER unfolding them separately, so a fold skews every later piece. The Bold Italic's tip came out a long slanted wedge. The & and @, the figure 8 (`figures.py`) and the italic g (`italic.py`) use `pieces=True`; none shows it today. That is a latent bug in `primitives.stroke`, left alone here.
3. **A plug into the foot poked out.** The 451 mark reached 0.03 x-height up into the foot.
   - A traced root leaves the baseline at the stroke's own angle, so its cut is wider than the stroke.
   - Where the foot is not flat on the line, the cut's corner showed beside the letter, as `instruments/ogonek_join.py` measures it: 503 square units at the italic ą, 461 at the Bold Italic Ą.
   - The A's leg is cut obliquely, the italic a's exit lifts off the line, and the Italic a's stem bottoms out 6 units ABOVE it.

**The placement that replaced it** (`build.py`, `footgeom` and the `OGO_TRACED` branch):
- **The flat top's x-range is matched to the foot's underside.** The mark's flat top is its top edge as an x-range, and each ogonek letter's foot is read as its underside, the lowest ink per unit column.
- **The search:** for each shift (2 units at a time, from −0.15 to +0.05 x-height) the mark's top goes to the highest point of that underside over its edge, never below the baseline. The letter's ink then lies over every unit of the edge. The lowest such mark wins, then the nearest.
- **Why no seam:** a composite is rasterized as one outline, so abutting contours leave no seam.
- **Results:**
  - Every ogonek touches its letter: gap 0, in all four cuts, all eight letters.
  - The A has less flat bottom than the root is wide, so its mark rides 1–5 units up its leg's underside (measured in "Verified" below). The result is slivers of 0–29 square units, under a tenth of a pixel at reading size.
  - The Italic ą hangs from a stem 6 units above the line, so its mark sits 6 units up, under that stem.

**Variants shown to the owner** (proof page):
- **P** (recommended, and RULED): the Pagella trace. It has the most hook: the tip curls back up, as round 451's ruling *"more hooky"* asked.
- **M:** the three-font mean, a smaller and more Times-like hook. Not chosen.
