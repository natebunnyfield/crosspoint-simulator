## 1. What changed

- **The default spacing is now B2.**
  - B2 is a ridge fit per style over glyph identity plus 38 measured shape
    features. It was refit at `e2b13e8` on 470 answers: the 370 bench
    judgments, 50 outlier-bench answers and active session 1.
  - Held out on the bench pairs it scores **10.37**, against **11.66** for
    round 395's tables and his repeatability of 10.83.
  - The tables are `tools/wedge_serif/outlines/spacing_b2.json`, written by
    `local_ai/b2_fit.py --extra`. They hold lowercase and punctuation bearings,
    395 roman and 447 italic kerns, and the holds.
- **The pairs he set by hand after the bench keep round 395's white exactly.**
  These are the kerns of rounds 384, 388, 389 and 390.
- **The g keeps round 340's fit.**
- **The Bold Italic `qj` clearance kern stays.** It is +4, B2 only, and exists
  because the refit's italic q right side (−4) would put that pair at
  0.0111 em, under the 0.012 floor. It now reads 0.0151.
- **`ALBO_SPACING_FIT`** now takes:
  - `b2`, the default (also used when unset);
  - `bench`, round 395's bench-fit tables (`ROM_LC_ADJ`, `ALD_LC_ADJ`,
    `*_PUNCT_FIT`, `_BENCH_PAIRS_*`), which are unchanged in the source;
  - anything else refuses to build.
