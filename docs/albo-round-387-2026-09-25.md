# Albo round 387: the eth redrawn, the bold italic Y's gap opened, four fractures (2026-09-25)

**Baseline:** HEAD `5de6923`, all four cuts built by `albo_build_all`, compared
against the round's build glyph by glyph. "Measured" below means read off the
built TTF. Coordinates are font units, with a cap of 674 and an x-height of 429.

The owner's rulings, 2026-09-25, given as answers to round 385/386's open
questions:

1. *"redraw blind pick"*: the italic ð reads as a Cyrillic б.
2. *"Open it to match"*: the Bold Italic Y's own gap is 1.3 units, the Italic's
   is 13.0.
3. *"Yes, all four"*: fix the fractures round 385 reported and left. These are
   the Bold Italic G, J and Ĳ, Ψ, and the italic R.
4. Recorded, no work: **italic arrows stay SLANTED.** The owner declined to make
   them upright.

## 1. The eth (`symbols2._crossed_eth`, all four cuts)

**What it was.** The o plus a "back" stroke. The back left the **bowl's top
left** and swept **right** to a flag above the bowl, and a flat bar sat on top
of it. That is how a Cyrillic б is built. The problem was in **every cut, the
roman included**, because one function draws all four. So the roman was fixed
too, as the brief allowed for "the same problem". Measured on the old Italic,
unsheared, the tip sat at 0.79–0.95 of the glyph's width (the right side), and
the ink reached 636.

**The references,** measured by `tools/wedge_serif/instruments/r387_eth_measure.py`.
Each face is unsheared by its measured slant (refs_registry.py) and scaled to
x-height 429:

| face | top | tip spans (fraction of width) | back at 1.15 xh | cross |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Flanker Griffo Italic | 659 | 0.18–0.33 | 0.62–0.77 | through the back at ~1.30 xh |
| Flanker Griffo Bold Italic | 649 | 0.16–0.36 | 0.54–0.76 | ~1.30 xh |
| Pagella Italic | 596 | 0.17–0.35 | 0.48–0.69 | diagonal |
| Coelacanth Italic | 734 | 0.00–0.20 | — | diagonal, high |
| Poetica | (its sxHeight is unreliable) | 0.00–0.42 | — | diagonal |

Georgia and Times, both roman and italic, were checked by eye.

**Every one builds it the same way.** The bowl's right wall rises out of the
bowl's widest point, leans back **left** over the bowl to a fine end above the
bowl's left third, and a short cross goes through the back a little above
halfway.

**What it is now.**
- **The join.** The ring's upper right, outside the back's centerline, is cut
  away. The back starts at the ring's widest point, on the wall's own measured
  width, with a vertical tangent. So wall and back are one contour edge with no
  step.
- **The back.** It rises to 0.89 ASC, leans left, and ends above the bowl's left
  edge with a square end 0.30 of the wall wide.
- **The cross.** A MATH-weight stroke at 0.42 of the way from x-height to the
  top, rising 0.10 of that height left to right, from 0.06 to 0.86 of the bowl
  width.

The new eth, measured:

|---|---|---|---|
| Italic | 661 | 0.00–0.24 | 0.49–0.67 |
| Bold Italic | 668 | 0.00–0.24 | 0.44–0.69 |
| Regular | 667 | 0.00–0.24 | 0.46–0.64 |

That is Flanker's top (659) and its lean. The tip sits a little further left
than Flanker's, which matches Coelacanth.

**Negatives, all built and looked at:**
- **The back laid over the whole o** with a vertical start (prototypes p1–p5).
  In the italic it swelled the right side to two walls wide, and after the 13°
  shear it stood upright and curled into a tight hook.
- **The back rooted inside the bowl at 0.30 h** (p6). The stroke ran through
  the counter, and its square start stuck out as a spur.
- **The back leaning in from its root** (p8). This gave a visible corner where
  it left the ring, because the tangents did not match.
- **A tip cut on the pen angle.** It read as a beak. The tip is now square.
- **A cross at 0.36** (p11b). At the Bold Italic its left end came close to the
  bowl's top left, a near-crack. 0.42 ships.
- **The upright "Georgia" back** (p7) was clean in the roman but a blob at the
  Bold. One construction for all four cuts was preferred to two.

## 2. The Bold Italic Y's gap (`aldine.py`, new `ALBO_ALD_Y_GAP_BOLD` = 0.040)

**Measured on HEAD** with `instruments/r387_y_gap.py`, unsheared. "Nearest"
means the closest the arm island comes to the spine.

| | nearest | narrowest horizontal white | rows with a gap (y/cap) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Italic | **13.0** | 15.6 at 0.42 | 0.38–0.43 |
| Bold Italic | **1.3** | 1.6 at 0.43 | 0.37–0.43 |

**Why it closes.** The mechanism is `Y_GAP`, round 354–355. It moves the arm's
ROOT 0.016 cap away from the spine and tapers that to nothing by the fourth
point. Above the Medium, `ALD_WF_UP` (1.38 at stem 116) widens both the spine
and the arm, and they grow toward each other, so the same displacement leaves
almost nothing.

**Ladder at the 700** (nearest approach):

| ALBO_ALD_Y_GAP_BOLD | 0.030 | 0.038 | 0.039 | **0.040** | 0.046 | 0.054 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| nearest | 8.7 | 12.6 | 12.6 | **13.5** | 16.0 | 19.9 |

**0.040 ships.**
- Nearest is 13.5 against the Italic's 13.0.
- The narrowest horizontal white is 17.6 at 0.43 cap, against the Italic's 15.6
  at 0.42.
- The gap runs from 0.37 to 0.43 cap, against the Italic's 0.38–0.43.
- The 400 is untouched. Its dial reads only when `pen.S > 84`.

**The yen keeps its ruled root.** `g_yen` draws `GLYPHS['Y']` and trims the
arm's root into round 386's option-b channel. With the Y's root moved, the first
build's Bold Italic ¥ showed a wedge of white between the bars. The fix:
- `g_yen` now asks for `GLYPHS['Y'](dict(c, yen=True))`.
- `a_Y` then uses `Y_ARM_YEN`, which is built from the 400's `Y_GAP`.
- The ¥ is outline-identical to HEAD in all four cuts. Its channel measures 14.0
  before and after.

**Other letters that might share the gap:**
- **The P's gap** is a different mechanism: a parallel-sided cut
  (`ALBO_ALD_P_GAP_*`), not a passing root. It already measures **6.8 at the
  Italic and 6.6 at the Bold Italic**, so it is consistent and was not touched.
- **The roman Y and P** have no gap. Their rows 0.30–0.44 are one run.
- **Nothing else uses `Y_GAP`** (grep).

**Glitch gate.** The Bold Italic Y was already `SPLIT` at HEAD, as 2 islands.
The 1.3-unit gap was still a gap. It stays SPLIT, and only the arm island's area
changes (34162 → 33372). This is the same accepted class as the Italic Y's
designed gap.

## 3. The four fractures

`cmp_jogs.py` locates them. Every fix uses `geom.ease_step`, round 385's tool:
the ink inside a box is replaced by its own convex hull. That turns a step into
a short slope and adds nothing outside the box. In every case the construction
is kept, and only the ink at the join moves.

### Bold Italic G (`aldine.a_G`)

- **Defect.** The spur starts square across its own direction where the arc ends
  on the pen cut, at a different width. The spur's lower-right corner stood 9.6
  units proud of the arc's outer edge, at (504, 75).
- **Fix.** A box on the OUTER side of the join only, from ex − 0.10 CS to
  ex + 0.80 CS. The counter's crotch on the inner side is untouched.
- **Scope.** Gated above stem 84: the Italic 400 reads 2.7 there, under
  visibility, and is left alone.
- **The construction is kept.** The arc, beak, spur and bar are all unchanged.
  Round 385's doc filed this under "R04, R05, round 238", but those rulings are
  on the ROMAN `caps_straight.g_G`. The italic G is the aldine `a_G`, and
  nothing in its drawing moved.

### J and Ĳ (`caps_straight.g_J`; the italic's `a_J` wraps it, so all four cuts)

- **Defect.** The stem arrives at its foot with its entasis, about 12 units
  wider than `CW` at the Bold. The hook started at `CW` 20 units lower, so both
  edges stepped in: 7.0 on the right and 5.0 on the left (Bold), 3.9 on the
  Regular Ĳ, 2.0 on the Regular J.
- **Fix.** The hook's starting width and center are read off the stem itself,
  1 unit above its foot. The remaining 1–2 units are eased over a box a quarter
  stem either side of the foot, and only 8 units wider than the stem.
- **Negative.** The first box was a whole stem wider. At the Bold Italic it
  reached the hook's tip, which the shear brings up within 54 units of the stem.
  It sealed the tip–stem slit into a hole: `cmp_aldine_glitch` CRACK, 750
  units², in J and Ĳ. The narrow box cannot reach the tip.

### Ψ (`symbols2.g_Psi`, all four cuts)

- **Defect.** Each arm is a capital stem stopping flat at 0.58 C. The curve that
  carries it in starts at a different width, so both edges stepped: 3.8 (R),
  6.0 (B), 5.7 (BI), 3.8 (It).
- **Fix.** Eased over a box one stem either side of each arm and 0.3 stem above
  and below the join. The cup's white is 288·k units inward.

### Italic R (`aldine.a_R`, Italic and Bold Italic)

The Poetica trace (round 135) is untouched. Three joins are eased:

1. **The top.** The stem's flat top stands at the cap line, above the bowl's top
   stroke (centered at 0.966 C). That left a 6.0-unit square step at (286, 670).
   The box covers C − 18 to C + 8, right of the stem's middle, so it stays above
   the counter.
2. **The knot** where the bowl's lower arm meets the leg's root. There was a V
   cut into the counter floor and a lip under the arm, 4.2 units, at (304, 327).
   - The box sits on the leg's first point.
   - Its lower reach is half a stem at the 400, capped at 46 units.
   - At the 700's half stem (66 units) the hull ran down the leg's left edge and
     made a heavy bracket. At 0.20 the lip stayed. Both were looked at; 46 is
     0.35 stem at the 700.
3. **The leg's bend,** where the swash turns from steep to flat. Its upper edge
   (the inside of the turn) kinked, 5.1 units, at (475, 79). The box covers the
   inside of the turn only; the swash's outer edge is unchanged.

The Bold Italic R carried the same knot visibly, although `cmp_jogs` read only
1.0 there, so both italics get the fix. The roman R is untouched. It still reads
2.0 (Regular) and 3.0 (Bold) at its top, which is not in this ruling.
