# Albo round 392: the reported leftovers (2026-09-25)

Owner 2026-09-25, *"subagent for all remaining work"*. The brief: fix the glyph
defects that earlier rounds reported and left, then run one fresh sweep for
fractures, steps and hairs in all four cuts.

- **Baseline:** round 391 (`80d8e74`), all four cuts built by `albo_build_all`.
- **Measurements:** read off the built TTFs, in font units (cap 674, x-height
  429). TTF coordinates in the italics are sheared. `cmp_jogs.py` is the
  finder throughout; its `dev` is the step's depth.
- **"Verified"** below means measured on the final build and looked at in a
  magnified before/after crop.

Every fix except two uses `geom.ease_step`, round 385's tool: the ink inside a
box is replaced by its own convex hull. That turns a step into a short slope
and adds nothing outside the box. The two exceptions are the roman ¥, which is
a cut, and the bold Q, which is a clip. No construction, dial or ruled drawing
moved.
