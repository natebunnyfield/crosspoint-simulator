# Albo round 470: the Regular's dot accents match the i's dot (2026-10-03)

The owner was asked "3 of 4" of the queued questions:
- Since round 467 shrank the tittle, the Regular's dieresis and dot-above
  stood 16% larger than the i's dot: 94.6 units against 81.6 as drawn ink,
  where before it was 5%.
- In the Bold they already match (145 against 143.5).
- They were already under the 4-dark-pixel floor at 10 pt on the X3, so
  matching would make them fainter.

**Ruling:** *"Match the i, ship."*

**Ships:** `ALBO_ACC_DOT_MATCH_I` 1 (`outlines/glyphs/accents.py`,
`_acc_dot_r`). In the roman Regular, the dieresis and the dot-above take the
i's own radius (`stems.TIT_RI` / `TIT_RI_ADJ`, whichever the i uses) through
the same `dot()` primitive, so they are the same dot. The Bold and the italics
are untouched. `ALBO_ACC_DOT_MATCH_I=0` builds round 469's.

## What changed

- **Regular dots, as drawn ink:** i 81.6, j 82.0, ė 82.0, ä 81.8 and 82.0
  units, where the accents had been 94.6.
- **Glyphs:** the 25 that carry them (ä ë ï ö ü ÿ ė ċ ġ ż and their capitals,
  İ, the spacing ¨ ˙ and the combining U+0307 / U+0308). The dieresis keeps
  its white between the two dots (`DIE_WHITE`), so the pair narrows with them.
