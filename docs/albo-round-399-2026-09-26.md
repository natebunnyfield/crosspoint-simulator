### The regularization

- **The shipped penalties (ALPHA_ID 1, ALPHA_F 30) were fixed on
  2026-09-26 before any curve was run.** They were not chosen by CV.
- **The curve is a broad, flat valley.** Every cell with ALPHA_ID 0.3–3 and
  ALPHA_F 30–300 lies within 9.90–10.07.
- **The minimum is (1, 100) at 9.90**, 0.12 under the shipped 10.02 (2
  shuffles).
  - That minimum was picked on the same held-out rows it is scored on, so the
    0.12 is optimistic.
  - It is also smaller than the shuffle-to-shuffle spread of the shipped CV
    number (about 0.3–0.4, `bench_fit --cv`).
- **Recommendation: keep (1, 30).** Moving to 100 would need a nested CV to
  earn it, and the curve says the most it could buy is about a tenth of a
  unit.
- **Low penalties are worse.** Shape-feature penalty 3 costs 0.3–0.7, and
  glyph penalty 10 costs 0.1–0.4. The fit is not under-regularized.

### Train against held-out, by subset

- **Overall:** in-sample 6.77, held-out 8.83, a **2.05-unit gap**.
- **His noise sets a floor** of about 7.66 for any model against one
  judgment. So the in-sample 6.77 sits a little **under** that floor, which
  is mild fitting of noise.
- **The held-out 8.83 is 1.2 over the floor.** That is the model's honest
  error.
- **The gap is largest on single-reading, high-residual pairs:** outlier
  bench 4.05, session 2 touched 4.29, session 4 touched 2.92.
- **It is smallest on skips** (0.7–2.3). Those are pairs the model already
  agreed with.
- **Why a pair can be fitted at all.** B2 has no per-pair parameter; a pair
  is fitted only through its two glyphs' bearings and the 38 shared features.
  So a large gap means **a rarely judged glyph**, for example the j, whose
  sides are carried by a handful of readings.
- **Held-out error per session, not in-sample, is what to watch.** Touched
  rows by session: 8.56 / 11.54 / 8.75 / 12.08. That is noisy and not
  worsening.
