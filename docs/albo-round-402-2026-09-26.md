## 2. The penalties: nested CV says keep (1, 30) [measured, `probe_alpha.py --nested`]

- **Setup.** The outer loop is bench_fit's first shuffle, 10 folds. Inside
  each outer fold, a 5-fold CV over its training bench pairs picks
  (ALPHA_ID, ALPHA_F) from the 5 × 5 grid. The pick is then scored on the
  outer fold it never saw.
- **Fixed (1, 30): 9.97. CV-chosen: 9.85.** Gain +0.13, paired standard
  error 0.09: about 1.4 standard errors, **not significant**.
- **The picks are stable.** It chose (3, 100) on 9 of 10 folds and (1, 100)
  on the other, i.e. a little more regularization.
- **Kept at (1, 30)**, per the instruction to change only if nested CV says
  so.
- **For the owner's "not overfitting" goal:** the evidence leans toward more
  regularization, not less. (3, 100) is the candidate if a larger bench ever
  makes the difference significant.

## 3. Clearance is measured now, not typed [new, `local_ai/clearance.py`]

- **Why.** The italic q's right side has moved further in at every refit:
  −4, −9, −9, −11. Each time a different set of q pairs fell under
  cmp_touch's 0.012 em floor, and was patched by hand in `kern.py`.

  copy) on every built cut. For each pair under the floor it writes the kern
  that lifts it to 0.015 em, into the table file's `clearance` block, keyed by
  cut. `kern.py` applies that block last, under the B2 switch.
