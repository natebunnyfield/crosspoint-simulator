Owner, on round 446 (TestFlight build 275): *"446 is terrible. please do this well, no shitty terminal"*.

## Diagnosis: the construction, not the dials

Rounds 441–446 adjusted dials on one construction, the drawn terminal (`R_TERM`, `_r_drawn_term`): a polygon whose outer edge is a free cubic to a tip T, with a straight face T → Lw and a straight underside Lw → I, where I is the arm's inner edge at the join.

Laid beside the references at one x-height (`ref_sheet.py`, r):
- **Every reference r** (Pagella, Coelacanth, Poetica, Flanker) carries the arm over a crest at the FAR RIGHT, and the end DROPS from it.
- **The white under the arm** is a smooth arch flowing out of the arm's inner edge. No notch.
- **Pagella** is the clearest pen edge: a short drop cut flat by the pen at about 0.75 xh.
- **Albo's r since F2** does the reverse. The end juts sideways as a wedge, with a V-notch cut under it and a point at its bottom.
- **A notch under a point reads as an open beak.** Every "beak", "shitty terminal" and "worse" since then was a dial move inside that shape.
- **The c's top** (rounds 434 / 436, approved) follows the reference structure: its underside leaves along the bowl's own edge. `albo-method.md` §1h says so ("drawn edges leave along the stroke's own edges"). The r's drawn terminal broke that rule at I.

## The construction

The end is the pen stroke itself. The snipped arm (`_r_snipped_arm`, `R_TERM=0`) carries the arm over the crest and DOWN, and stops. New dials, all byte-identical at default (verified on the live builder against HEAD):
- **`R_SOH1` / `R_SOH2`** (0.45): the over-cubic's handles, times the chord. Out along the climb, back from the end. Long handles carry the arm high over a crest before it turns.
- **`R_SCAP`** (0): with 1, no file. The stroke stops at E and its own square cap is the flat cut. Arriving at E travelling `R_SEA`, the cap sits square to that travel.
- **`R_SABS`** (0): with 1, the arm's thin width keys are held at the arc length round 441's path put them. A longer path otherwise drags the root's thickening up the climb (the tooth H1/H2 left at the stem).
- **`R_SCLIP`** (3.0): the file's reach past the face. Kept for the filed variants; not used with `R_SCAP`.
- **Hair removal:** with `R_SCAP`, a 1.5-unit opening with MITRED joins removes the hair the pen's inner edge folds into at the inner apex (the Bold Italic's heavier hairline made one) and gives every real corner back sharp.
- **The arm:** keeps the n's hairline (`R_WEDGE` 1, the width ramping only from `R_WSTART` 0.70–0.72, past the crest's start).

**Candidates** (`ALBO_ALD_R_*`; Bold Italic in brackets, `_700`). Common to all: `TERM` 0, `ENDHULL` 0, `CHAMF` 0, `SCAP` 1, `SABS` 1, `WEDGE` 1.

## Negative results (this round)

- **A drop drawn with the drawn terminal** (outer cubic over the crest down to a bottom-right tip, a near-horizontal face, a cubic underside; K1–K3): the free outer cubic bulges into a round lobe, which was ruled out on 2026-09-29.
- **The snipped arm cut square across a DOWNWARD end with the file** (`SCAP` 0): the file's half-plane plus disc removes the arm's own climb, which passes within its reach below the cut line (S1–S3, E1–E4). With a small reach it leaves the overshoot floating (D1–D4). Stop the stroke instead (`SCAP`).
- **Widening from `WSTART` 0.45** thickens the arm from mid-climb and flattens its contrast, which the owner corrected twice (rounds 442, 446).
- **A tight turn into the drop** folds the pen's inner edge into a curl (F1–F4). Loosen it with a longer descent (`SOH2` 0.85–0.95), an arrival off vertical (`SEA` −70 to −85), and widening that starts after the turn.
