## What the first try got wrong

Round 483 read the word-images finding (4.1: the u is not the n turned over)
as a structural fix and mirrored the n's arch. That moves the bowl's weight up
the right stem, and the owner reads the u by where its weight sits. The
references' u's join LOW too (bowl rise 74-122 units at a 429 x-height; Albo's
92 is inside); what differs is their n, whose arch is shallow (78-128 below
the x-line) where Albo's is deep (188, ruled in round 93). So the number the
finding flagged -- the u's middle band at 0.938 of the n's against the
references' 0.995-1.030 -- is mostly the n's.
