# Albo round 393: the Bold Italic j head, tracking c ships, and the roman R balanced to the bold (2026-09-25)

Three owner rulings on 2026-09-25:

1. The Bold Italic j head (round 392's "found and NOT fixed", proof 16):
   *"Fix it, just ship"*.
2. Spacing: *"c · +6 (caps +3)"*. Round 388's `ALBO_TRACK` arm c becomes
   the shipped default.
3. *"The Roman R should match the optical balance of the bold capital R."*

- **Baseline:** round 392 (`6ec8517`), all four cuts built by
  `build_env.sh albo_build_all`.
- **Units:** font units (cap 674, x-height 429). Italic numbers are read
  unsheared (tan 13° = 0.2309) where the text says so.
- **Verified** means measured on the final build and looked at in a magnified
  before/after render.

## 1. The Bold Italic j head [verified]

**Cause.** The j's head is `wedge_head` (aldine.py). It sizes its length and
width off `S`. The j's body sizes its widths off the reference table times
`ALD_WF_UP`. From the 400 to the 700, the head grew 1.73× (S 66.9 → 116) while
the stem it sits on grew only 1.38× (`ALD_WF_UP`). The head's widest point
(`HEAD_W` × reversed profile 2.05) went from 79 units to 138. Its outline,
dumped from the builder in design space:

| | head top | stem's cut top at that x | head right end | stem's right edge there |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Italic (400) | 410 | 422–436 | (282, 380) | 283 |
| Bold Italic, round 392 | **440** | 420–438 | **(333, 384)** | 325 |
| Bold Italic, round 393 | 426 | 429–433 | (323, 382) | 325 |

At the 400 the head lies inside the stem at the top and on the right. It shows
only as the entry nub on the left. At the 700 its upper edge ran along the
stem's slanted cut top and crossed it several times, which made the
**crumple**. Its right end stood 8 units out of the stem, which made the
**spur**. A hull cannot remove either one.

**Fix.** `a_j` now draws the head at `J_HEAD_REF_S × ALD_WF_UP` instead of at
`S`. Length and width both take the factor
`_jk = ALD_WF_UP · min(S, J_HEAD_REF_S) / S`. `J_HEAD_REF_S` is 66.9, the stem
the 400 was judged at, and it has an env dial.

- At the 400 the factor is exactly 1.0, so the Italic j is outline-identical.
- `J_HEAD_LEAN` 0.60 and `J_HEAD_LEN` 0.62 are unchanged. The head still leans
  left and is still shortened with the lean. Its left nub now stands 10 units
  off the stem, against the Italic's 8, which is the same proportion.
- No hull and no clip was needed.

**Follows.** The fix moved these Bold Italic glyphs:

- j, ȷ, ĵ, ĳ
- the PUA fj (U+E002), which is drawn but not substituted

Contour counts are unchanged, so no `PHASE_LEGACY_STYLE` entry was needed.

**Advances.** The fitter reads the head's nub as the j's left ink. The nub moved
13 units in, so the fitted advances moved:

| glyph | round 392 | round 393 |
|---|---|---|
| j | 244 | 231 |
| ȷ, ĵ | 265 | 256 |
| ĳ | 699 | 689 |
| fj | 856 | 847 |

These are the arm-a figures. Tracking c then adds 6 to each (§2).

**One consequence, fixed.** With the j 13 units further left, its tail reached
in under the q's descender. `qj` went from 0.0150 to 0.0081 em, under the
0.012 floor. A descender clash is a kern pair (round 178), so the 700's `qj`
takes a further **+7**, which puts it back at 0.0151 em (`kern.py`, the
italic's stem-84 block). No other j pair came within the floor. The nearest
were 4j at 0.0167 and Rj at 0.0196.

## 3. The roman R, balanced to the bold [verified]

**Measured.** Measured on the bowl's and leg's CENTRELINES, from the stem's
midline, in cap heights. The centreline removes the weight difference.

| | bowl reaches | bowl floor (lower stroke's centre) | leg springs at | leg tip | leg past bowl | leg ink / bowl ink |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Regular, round 392 | **0.647** | **0.483** | 0.312 | 0.730 | **0.083** | **0.611** |
| Bold | 0.549 | 0.500 | 0.292 | 0.730 | 0.181 | 0.727 |
| Regular, round 393 | 0.549 | 0.500 | 0.292 | 0.730 | 0.181 | 0.724 |

Other measurements (`measR.py`, session scratch):

- Leg angle between 0.15 and 0.35 cap: 62.5° Regular, 63.2° Bold. Unchanged,
  and already matched.
- Leg width against stem: 1.19 and 1.10, the same in both.
- Counter width/height: 1.20 → 1.02. The Bold's is 0.96. The remaining gap is
  the weight: a thinner stroke leaves a larger counter.

**Cause.** The Regular's leg was already the Bold's. `R_KICK_REACH` is
absolute. The bowl was not the Bold's, and the reason is the width solver, not
the drawing:

- `solve_widths` scales `W['R']` until the R's ink width reaches its
  reference.
- The R's widest ink is the leg tip, which does not move with W.
- So at the 400 the solver can never get there. It ran W up to 1.403, heading
  for its 1.45 clamp, against the Bold's 1.084.
- That inflated only the bowl: 18% wider than the Bold's, and lower. The letter
  read top-heavy, with the leg tucked under it.

**Fix** (`caps_straight.py`, `R_BAL`). Under stem 84 the bowl is placed by the
Bold's own centreline numbers instead of by W:

- `R_BAL_REACH` 0.549: the bowl's centreline right extreme.
- `R_BAL_FLOOR` 0.500: the lower stroke's centreline.
- `R_BAL_JOINX` 0.292: where the leg springs. That is −63° round the
  Regular's bowl, against `R_JOIN`'s −66°.

What did not move:

- The Bold, above stem 84, is outline-identical.
- The leg's path, the round-387 and round-392 cap-top ease (`_ease_cap_top`),
  and the R13 counter clip are all unchanged.

Other results:

- The R's advance is unchanged at 664, because the fitter reads the leg.
- Ŕ Ř Ŗ follow as composites, and ® moved with it.
- `R_BAL=0` gives round 392's R.

**The Italic R: not changed.** It does not have the imbalance. Measured
unsheared, Italic against Bold Italic:

| | Italic | Bold Italic |
|---|---|---|
| bowl reach from the stem midline | 0.449 C | 0.470 C (the Italic's bowl is if anything narrower) |
| bowl floor | 0.455 C | 0.455 C |
| leg past the bowl | 0.29 C | 0.27 C |
| leg/bowl ink | 0.816 | 0.765 |

No top-heaviness to correct.

**R pairs.** `cmp_touch` found nothing in the R rows on any cut. `cmp_jogs` on
R Ŕ Ř Ŗ shows only the stem-foot bracket at 1.0, as before. So no kerns were
added.
