# Albo round 427 -- the fences sit symmetrically about every letter (2026-09-28)

Owner: *"improve letter spacing with parentheses and all brackets. currently not symmetrical"*.

**Why they were not.** The fences `( ) [ ] { }` take symmetric bearings on their own ink (`build.fit`, `PUNCT_FENCES`), but the letter inside brings its own two sides: `(n` sees n's LEFT, `n)` its RIGHT, and Albo's letters carry different left and right bearings. Measured on round 426 as the closest approach inside the fence across the letter's band (`instruments/fence_gap.py`: HarfBuzz-shaped, kerned, rendered at 1000 px, the row-wise white between the two inks), |inside the close - inside the open| per letter: Regular mean 11.8 (p90 30), Italic 30.5 (p90 58), Bold 16.0 (35), BoldItalic 32.1 (58).

**The fix** (`local_ai/fences.py`, applied by `kern.py` from `spacing_b2.json`'s new `fences` block, per cut, before clearance; `ALBO_FENCES=0` builds without it): for every fence pair and every letter and figure, the open pair takes +d/2 and the close pair -d/2 (d = close - open), so `(x` equals `x)` and each letter keeps its own looseness (its fitted spacing). Kerns under 4 units dropped, capped at 40. Letters whose difference exceeds 80 are deliberate special kerns (the Q's tail, the J) and are skipped. 208 / 308 / 212 / 320 kerns (Regular / Italic / Bold / BoldItalic).

After: |close - open| mean 7.2 / 6.6 / 6.3 / 6.0, p90 4.0 / 4.5 / 4.0 / 2.0 (the remaining maxima are the skipped Q and J).

**The measure is the closest approach, not the mean white**: the mean counts a letter's own open white as gap (c reads 399 on its open right against 218 on its left; E, L, F worse) -- the spacing method's rule that a letter's open white belongs to the letter. The first solve used the mean and produced a 225-unit kern; recorded so it is not retried.
