Round 453 set every italic mark where the REFERENCES set theirs (it added their per-letter medians to its axis, `glyphs/mark_axis_it.py` B): the E's marks well left of the box's centre, over the stem side, the A's right of it, over the apex. That is what the owner calls uncentred, and he asked for the box as the reference instead. **So the references' per-letter offsets are dropped**: the box is the rule.

**The rule** (`outlines/build.py` `stem_axis_line`, `ALBO_MARK_BOX`, the italics only): the axis is the box's centre line; every symmetric mark's ink centroid goes on it at its own height; the acute, grave and double acute keep only their lean (`mark_axis_it.py` M: +0.041 / −0.063 / +0.079 lowercase, +0.057 / −0.097 / +0.107 capitals), which is how much further right (left) the references set those marks than a symmetric one on the same letter. `ALBO_MARK_BOX=0` restores round 453.

**Over an ascender** (h, l) the band is the ascender's straight stem, 0.25-0.55 of the x-height below its top: a first build took 0.15-0.85 of the ascender, its band reached the head serif, which juts left, and set ĥ and ĺ 0.09 of the x-height left. With the stem band their marks place exactly as in round 453 (composite offsets identical in both italics).

**The Greek tonos moves with it** (ά έ ή ί ό ύ ώ are italic marks too): every one now sits at the acute's lean, +0.04 of the x-height, right of its box's centre; ή and ύ had been +0.13 (far right). Moves, Italic: ά +42, έ +39, ί +30, ό +12, ώ +1, ή −35, ύ −40 units. The Greek capitals with tonos are not encoded.

**Result**: every symmetric mark within 0.02 of its box's centre in both italics (most within 0.005; the z −0.022 / −0.027, its diagonal's extremes at the band's ends). Marks moved, Italic, in units: R +83, E +58, a +43, A −38, g +36, z +35, C −34, e +30, n s S Z +25 to +27, o u i O U N I under 10. ĥ ĺ unchanged.
